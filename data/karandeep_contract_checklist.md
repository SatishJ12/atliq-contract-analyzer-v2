# Contract checklist (Karandeep — personal notes)

*(Found in Karandeep's Google Keep; the list itself was last edited March 2024. Shared with Jay once "so you know what I look for". The only written record of how AtliQ reviews contracts.)*

things I check before signing —

- LDs → cap it. never open ended
- liability → 1x fees. NOT unlimited
- indemnity → mutual. we don't cover their mistakes
- payment → 30 days, 45 max
- gov law → ours (India for Pvt Ltd, US for Inc)
- IP → client owns what we build for them, we keep our own stuff
- termination → they pay for work done
- confidentiality → mutual, fine usually
- NDAs → just sign if mutual

check SOW matches proposal (scope, milestones, rates)

*Internal note (Karandeep, September 2026, added after the 22 Sep contract-queue meeting):*

*This list is two years old and was never complete. It says nothing about the things that actually worry me now — what we've already promised other clients. Non-competes, exclusivity, pricing promises, who owns what we reused. None of that is in the contract in front of me; it's in contracts I signed a year or three ago and don't remember clause by clause. I review everything myself, usually after 10 pm, and the only "system" is my memory of how the last negotiation went. When I'm rushed I sign things I would never have agreed to on a normal day — Brightwater proved that. We are doing nine contracts a month now. This doesn't scale and it isn't safe.*

*And the list only covers one side of the table. Half of what I sign now isn't a client contract at all — NDAs, partnership agreements with platform companies, and our own agreements with agencies and freelancers we bring in. When we're the buyer, the same clause I'd reject from a client is sitting in our own template. I'd rather sign a mutual NDA than a one-sided one, and I'd rather a deal work for both sides than win it at the other side's expense — a six-person agency or a freelancer shouldn't get our paper and feel the way I felt reading Brightwater's. Nothing written down says any of that either.*
