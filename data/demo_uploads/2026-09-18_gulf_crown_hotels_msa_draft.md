## 2. RECITALS

2.1 WHEREAS the Company owns and operates a portfolio of hotels and resorts within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and wishes to modernise its commercial and guest-facing operations in line with its Digital Transformation Programme;

2.2 WHEREAS the Service Provider has represented that it possesses the requisite expertise, qualified personnel and experience in data platforms, analytics and artificial intelligence to design, build and deploy the Platform described herein;

2.3 AND WHEREAS the Company, relying upon the representations of the Service Provider, wishes to engage the Service Provider, and the Service Provider wishes to be engaged, upon the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties, having full legal capacity to contract, have agreed as follows.

## 3. DEFINITIONS

3.1 "**Platform**" means the revenue management and guest analytics platform to be delivered by the Service Provider under this Agreement, as further described in Clause 4 and Schedule 1.
3.2 "**Properties**" means the hotels and resorts owned or operated by the Company and listed in Schedule 2.
3.3 "**Deliverables**" means all software, dashboards, models, documentation, reports and other materials to be provided by the Service Provider under this Agreement.
3.4 "**Milestone**" means each stage of the Services identified in Schedule 3, together with its associated completion date.
3.5 "**Contract Value**" means the total fees payable under this Agreement, being One Hundred and Forty Thousand United States Dollars (USD 140,000).
3.6 "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Friday, Saturday or official public holiday in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
3.7 "**Confidential Information**" means all information disclosed by one Party to the other, whether oral, written or electronic, that is designated as confidential or that ought reasonably to be understood as confidential.
3.8 "**Guest Data**" means any personal data relating to guests, members of the Company's loyalty programme, or prospective guests of the Properties.

## 4. SCOPE OF SERVICES

4.1 The Service Provider shall design, develop, test, deploy and support a revenue management and guest analytics platform for the Company's nine (9) Properties located in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, as listed in Schedule 2.
4.2 The Platform shall include, at a minimum, the following components:
   (a) **Revenue Management Module** — demand forecasting at room-type and night level, dynamic rate recommendations, competitor rate ingestion, pickup and pace analysis, and a length-of-stay and overbooking optimisation engine;
   (b) **Guest Analytics Module** — a unified guest profile consolidating data from the Company's property management system, central reservation system, loyalty programme and point-of-sale systems; guest segmentation; lifetime value scoring; and propensity models for upsell and repeat stay;
   (c) **Executive and Property Dashboards** — Arabic and English dashboards for the Chief Commercial Officer, Revenue Managers and General Managers of each Property;
   (d) **Integrations** — integration with the Company's existing property management system, channel manager and CRM at each Property;
   (e) **Training and Handover** — training for up to forty (40) Company personnel across the Properties.
4.3 The Service Provider shall perform the Services in accordance with the specifications set out in Schedule 1, the Milestones set out in Schedule 3, and any reasonable instructions issued by the Company's Project Director.
4.4 The Service Provider shall deploy a dedicated project manager who shall be available during Riyadh business hours and shall attend on-site at the Company's head office as reasonably requested.
4.5 Any change to the scope of Services shall be agreed in writing through a change request signed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 In consideration of the Services, the Company shall pay the Service Provider the Contract Value in accordance with the payment schedule in Schedule 3.
5.2 The Service Provider shall submit an invoice upon the Company's written acceptance of each Milestone. Invoices shall be addressed to the Company's Finance Department and shall reference the Agreement Reference number.
5.3 The Company shall pay each undisputed invoice within sixty (60) days from the date of receipt of a valid invoice.
5.4 All fees are exclusive of Value Added Tax. Where the Company is required by law to withhold any tax from payments, the Company shall deduct such tax and provide the Service Provider with the relevant withholding certificate.
5.5 The Company may dispute any invoice in good faith by written notice within fifteen (15) Business Days of receipt, and the Parties shall endeavour to resolve such dispute amicably.
5.6 All payments shall be made in United States Dollars by bank transfer to the account nominated by the Service Provider in writing.

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of thirty-six (36) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Clause 6.
6.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy such breach within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice thereof.
6.3 The Company may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice, in which event the Company shall pay for all Services performed and accepted up to the date of termination.
6.4 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other Party becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation or makes any arrangement with its creditors.
6.5 Upon termination, the Service Provider shall deliver to the Company all Deliverables and work in progress, and shall provide reasonable transition assistance for a period of up to sixty (60) days.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other Party and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, save to its employees, advisers and subcontractors who need to know it for the purposes of this Agreement and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less stringent than those herein.
7.2 The obligations in this Clause 7 shall not apply to information which is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement, was lawfully in the receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure, or is required to be disclosed by law or by a competent authority.
7.3 The obligations in this Clause 7 shall survive for a period of five (5) years following the expiry or termination of this Agreement.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 All Deliverables specifically created for the Company under this Agreement shall, upon payment in full, become the property of the Company.
8.2 The Service Provider shall retain ownership of its pre-existing tools, methodologies and know-how ("**Background IP**"). To the extent any Background IP is incorporated into the Deliverables, the Service Provider grants the Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use such Background IP solely as part of the Deliverables for the Company's internal business purposes.
8.3 The Company shall retain all rights in its data, including Guest Data, and in any materials provided by the Company to the Service Provider.

   (c) it has the full right, power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; and
   (d) it is not bound by any agreement or restriction which would prevent or restrict it from performing the Services for the Company.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, special or consequential loss, or loss of profits, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
10.2 Save in respect of liability arising from fraud, wilful misconduct, breach of Clause 7 (Confidentiality) or Clause 13 (Data Protection), each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the Contract Value.

## 11. INDEMNITY

11.1 The Service Provider shall indemnify the Company against all losses, claims and expenses arising from any claim by a third party that the Deliverables infringe the intellectual property rights of such third party.
11.2 The Company shall indemnify the Service Provider against claims arising from the Service Provider's use, in accordance with this Agreement, of materials supplied by the Company.

## 12. DELAY PENALTIES

12.1 Time shall be of the essence in respect of the Milestone dates set out in Schedule 3.
12.2 Should the Service Provider fail to achieve any Milestone by its scheduled completion date, the Service Provider shall pay to the Company, by way of liquidated damages and not as a penalty, an amount equal to one percent (1%) of the Contract Value for each week or part thereof of delay.
12.3 The aggregate liquidated damages payable under this Clause 12 shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the Contract Value.
12.4 The Company may deduct any liquidated damages due from any amount payable to the Service Provider under this Agreement.
12.5 The Service Provider shall not be liable for delay to the extent such delay is caused by a Force Majeure Event as defined in Clause 15.

## 13. DATA PROTECTION

13.1 The Service Provider shall process Guest Data only on the documented instructions of the Company and in compliance with the Personal Data Protection Law issued by Royal Decree No. M/19 and its Implementing Regulations, and any other applicable law in the Kingdom.
13.2 Guest Data shall be hosted within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia unless the Company provides prior written approval for any transfer outside the Kingdom.
13.3 The Service Provider shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Guest Data and shall notify the Company of any personal data breach within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws and regulations in force in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
14.2 The Parties shall seek to resolve any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement amicably through negotiation between senior executives within thirty (30) days.
14.3 Failing amicable resolution, the dispute shall be referred to the competent courts in the City of Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, which shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 15. GENERAL PROVISIONS

15.1 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations where such failure results from events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, epidemic or governmental action ("**Force Majeure Event**").
15.2 **Assignment.** The Service Provider shall not assign or subcontract any of its obligations without the prior written consent of the Company.
15.3 **Language.** This Agreement is executed in English and Arabic. In the event of any inconsistency, the Arabic text shall prevail.
15.4 **Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier or email to the addresses set out in Clause 1, marked for the attention of the Company's Chief Technology Officer (Mr. Faisal Al-Otaibi) and the Service Provider's Chief Executive Officer respectively.
15.5 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with its Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior negotiations and understandings.
15.6 **Amendment.** No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.
15.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
