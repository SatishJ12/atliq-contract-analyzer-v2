# MASTER PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Harrington Health Systems, Inc. — Contract No. HHS-IT-2026-0193**
**Form: HHS Vendor MPSA (Rev. 07/2026)**

This Master Professional Services Agreement (together with all Exhibits, Statements of Work and amendments hereto, this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the date of the last signature below (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Harrington Health Systems, Inc.**, a Texas nonprofit corporation with its principal place of business at 6400 Fannin Street, Suite 1800, Houston, Texas 77030 ("**Harrington**" or "**Client**"), and **AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Contractor**"). Client and Contractor are each a "**Party**" and collectively the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Client operates an integrated health system comprising acute care hospitals, ambulatory surgery centers, and outpatient clinics in the greater Houston metropolitan area;

WHEREAS, Client desires to engage Contractor to design and implement an enterprise clinical and operational analytics platform, and Contractor desires to provide such services, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.
1.2 "**Applicable Law**" means all federal, state and local laws, rules, regulations, ordinances and binding guidance applicable to a Party or the Services, including HIPAA, the HITECH Act, the Texas Medical Records Privacy Act (Tex. Health & Safety Code ch. 181), and the Anti-Kickback Statute.
1.3 "**Business Associate Agreement**" or "**BAA**" means the business associate agreement attached hereto as Exhibit D.
1.4 "**Client Materials**" means all data, information, systems access, documents and other materials furnished by or on behalf of Client.
1.5 "**Contractor Personnel**" means all employees, agents, subcontractors and independent contractors of Contractor or its subcontractors who perform any portion of the Services.
1.6 "**Deliverables**" means all work product, documentation, software, code, models, reports, dashboards, data pipelines, configurations and other materials prepared, developed or delivered by or on behalf of Contractor under this Agreement, in whole or in part.
1.7 "**Delivery Date**" means each date for delivery or completion of a Deliverable or Milestone set forth in an applicable SOW.
1.8 "**Fees**" means the amounts payable to Contractor as set forth in an SOW.
1.9 "**Losses**" means any and all losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, claims, actions, judgments, settlements, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs or expenses of whatever kind, including reasonable attorneys' fees.
1.10 "**PHI**" has the meaning given to "protected health information" under 45 C.F.R. § 160.103.
1.11 "**Pre-Existing Materials**" means any software, tools, libraries, frameworks, templates, methodologies, accelerators, know-how and other materials owned or licensed by Contractor prior to the Effective Date or developed independently of this Agreement.
1.12 "**Services**" means the services described in Article 2 and in each SOW.
1.13 "**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by the Parties that references this Agreement.
1.14 "**Total Contract Value**" means the aggregate Fees payable under this Agreement and all SOWs issued hereunder, which as of the Effective Date is Two Hundred Ten Thousand U.S. Dollars ($210,000.00).

## ARTICLE 2 — SERVICES

2.1 **Scope.** Contractor shall perform the Services described in the initial SOW attached as Exhibit A (the "**Initial SOW**"), which include: (a) design and build of a cloud data platform consolidating data from Client's electronic health record, patient accounting, scheduling and workforce management systems; (b) development of operational dashboards for bed management, emergency department throughput, operating room utilization and staffing; (c) a readmission-risk predictive model for use by Client's care management team; and (d) knowledge transfer to Client's Enterprise Analytics department.
2.2 **Additional SOWs.** The Parties may from time to time execute additional SOWs. Each SOW shall be governed by this Agreement. In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and an SOW, this Agreement shall control unless the SOW expressly states the specific Section of this Agreement it is intended to modify.
2.3 **Standards of Performance.** Contractor shall perform the Services in a diligent, professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the highest standards of the industry, and in compliance with Applicable Law and Client's policies made available to Contractor, as updated from time to time.
2.4 **Contractor Personnel.** Contractor shall assign qualified Contractor Personnel. Client may, in its sole discretion, request removal of any Contractor Personnel, and Contractor shall promptly replace such individual at no additional cost to Client. Contractor Personnel accessing Client facilities or systems shall complete Client's background check, drug screening, immunization and compliance training requirements.
2.5 **Subcontracting.** Contractor shall not subcontract any portion of the Services without Client's prior written consent. Contractor shall remain fully responsible for any subcontractor's performance.
2.6 **Client Cooperation.** Client shall use reasonable efforts to provide Contractor with access to Client Materials reasonably necessary for the performance of the Services.
2.7 **Change Orders.** No change in the scope of Services shall be binding unless set forth in a written change order executed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

## ARTICLE 3 — ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Client shall have thirty (30) days following delivery of each Deliverable to test and evaluate such Deliverable against the acceptance criteria in the applicable SOW.
3.2 If Client determines that a Deliverable does not conform, Client shall notify Contractor, and Contractor shall correct the non-conformity at no cost within ten (10) business days. Client may repeat the acceptance process until the Deliverable conforms, or may, at its option, reject the Deliverable and receive a refund of all Fees paid in respect thereof.
3.3 Acceptance shall not be deemed to occur by use in production, by payment, or by the passage of time.

## ARTICLE 4 — FEES, INVOICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Client shall pay Contractor the Fees set forth in each SOW. Fees are firm and fixed and shall not be increased during the term of the applicable SOW.
4.2 **Invoices.** Contractor shall invoice Client monthly in arrears for Deliverables accepted in the preceding month. Each invoice shall reference the Contract No. and SOW number, and be submitted through Client's supplier portal. Invoices not submitted within ninety (90) days after acceptance shall be deemed waived.
4.3 **Payment Terms.** Client shall pay each properly submitted, undisputed invoice within ninety (90) days after Client's receipt thereof.
4.4 **Disputed Amounts.** Client may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith. Contractor shall continue performing the Services during the pendency of any dispute.
4.5 **Set-off.** Client may set off against any amount owed to Contractor any amount owed by Contractor to Client under this Agreement or any other agreement.
4.6 **Expenses.** Client shall reimburse only pre-approved, reasonable travel expenses incurred in accordance with Client's Vendor Travel Policy (Exhibit C).
4.7 **Taxes.** Client is exempt from certain sales and use taxes and shall provide an exemption certificate upon request. Contractor shall be responsible for all taxes based on its income or personnel.

## ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 **Term.** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for three (3) years, unless earlier terminated as provided herein. Each SOW shall remain in effect for the period stated therein.
5.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after written notice thereof.
5.3 **Termination for Convenience.** Client may terminate this Agreement or any SOW, in whole or in part, for any reason or no reason, upon seven (7) days' written notice to Contractor. Upon such termination, Client's sole obligation shall be to pay Fees for Deliverables accepted by Client prior to the effective date of termination; Client shall have no obligation to pay for work in progress, unaccepted Deliverables, demobilization costs or anticipated profits.
5.4 **Termination for Regulatory Reasons.** Client may terminate immediately if Contractor or any Contractor Personnel is excluded from participation in any federal health care program.
5.5 **Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination, Contractor shall (a) immediately deliver to Client all Deliverables, whether complete or incomplete, and all Client Materials; (b) provide transition assistance as reasonably requested by Client for up to ninety (90) days at the rates in the applicable SOW; and (c) certify in writing the return or destruction of all PHI in accordance with the BAA.
5.6 **Survival.** Articles 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14 and 15 shall survive expiration or termination.

## ARTICLE 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by a Party, including business plans, financial information, patient information, trade secrets and Client Materials.
6.2 Each Party shall (a) hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence, (b) use it only to perform or receive the Services, and (c) disclose it only to those employees and contractors with a need to know who are bound by written obligations at least as protective.
6.3 The foregoing obligations shall not apply to information that (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was rightfully known without restriction prior to disclosure; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.
6.4 A receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, provided it gives prompt prior notice (where legally permitted) and cooperates in seeking protective treatment.
6.5 Contractor acknowledges that any breach of this Article may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and that Client shall be entitled to injunctive relief without the posting of bond.

7.2 **Assignment.** To the extent any Deliverable, or any Pre-Existing Materials incorporated into or necessary for the use of any Deliverable, does not qualify as a work made for hire, Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers and conveys to Client all right, title and interest therein, including all intellectual property rights, without further consideration.
7.3 **Further Assurances.** Contractor shall execute such documents and take such actions as Client may reasonably request to perfect, register or enforce Client's rights under this Article, and hereby appoints Client as its attorney-in-fact for such purpose.
7.4 **Moral Rights.** To the extent permitted by law, Contractor waives, and shall cause Contractor Personnel to waive, all moral rights in the Deliverables.
7.5 **Client Materials.** All Client Materials are and shall remain the sole property of Client. Nothing in this Agreement grants Contractor any right in Client Materials except the limited right to use them to perform the Services.
7.6 **Third-Party Components.** Contractor shall not incorporate any open-source or third-party software into any Deliverable without Client's prior written approval.

8.1 Each Party represents and warrants that it is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing, and has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.
8.2 Contractor further represents and warrants that: (a) the Services shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner; (b) the Deliverables shall conform to the specifications in the applicable SOW for a period of twelve (12) months following acceptance; (c) the Deliverables shall not infringe, misappropriate or violate any third-party intellectual property right; (d) the Deliverables shall not contain any virus, malicious code, time bomb or disabling device; (e) neither Contractor nor any Contractor Personnel is excluded, debarred or suspended from any federal health care program; and (f) Contractor shall comply with all Applicable Law.
8.3 Contractor shall, at Client's option, re-perform any non-conforming Services or refund the Fees paid therefor.

## ARTICLE 9 — INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 **By Contractor.** Contractor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Client, its Affiliates, and their respective trustees, directors, officers, employees, medical staff, agents, successors and assigns (the "**Client Indemnitees**") from and against any and all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) the Services or Deliverables, (b) any breach of this Agreement or the BAA by Contractor, (c) any claim that a Deliverable infringes any third-party intellectual property right, or (d) any act or omission of Contractor Personnel, in each case regardless of whether such Losses are caused in whole or in part by the negligence of any Client Indemnitee.
9.2 **By Client.** To the extent permitted by law, Client shall indemnify Contractor against third-party claims to the extent arising from Client's gross negligence or willful misconduct.
9.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, provided that failure to do so shall not relieve the indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent materially prejudiced. Client shall have the right to participate with counsel of its choice at Contractor's expense. Contractor shall not settle any claim without Client's prior written consent.

## ARTICLE 10 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** IN NO EVENT SHALL CLIENT BE LIABLE TO CONTRACTOR FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, EXEMPLARY OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.
10.2 **Cap on Client Liability.** CLIENT'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES ACTUALLY PAID BY CLIENT TO CONTRACTOR UNDER THIS AGREEMENT.
10.3 **Contractor Liability.** NOTHING IN THIS AGREEMENT SHALL LIMIT OR EXCLUDE CONTRACTOR'S LIABILITY FOR ANY LOSSES, WHETHER DIRECT OR INDIRECT, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, INCLUDING CONTRACTOR'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, BREACH OF THE BAA, OR ANY DATA SECURITY INCIDENT.

## ARTICLE 11 — DELAY; LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

11.1 **Time of the Essence.** Time is of the essence with respect to each Delivery Date.
11.2 **Liquidated Damages.** The Parties acknowledge that Client's damages from delay would be difficult to ascertain. Accordingly, if Contractor fails to achieve any Delivery Date, Contractor shall pay Client, as liquidated damages, an amount equal to two percent (2%) of the Total Contract Value for each calendar day from the Delivery Date until the date the applicable Deliverable is accepted.
11.3 **Cumulative Remedies.** Liquidated damages under this Article are in addition to and not in lieu of Client's right to recover its actual damages, to terminate this Agreement for cause, and to exercise any other right or remedy available at law or in equity. Client may deduct liquidated damages from any amounts otherwise due to Contractor.
11.4 **Notice of Delay.** Contractor shall notify Client in writing within two (2) business days of becoming aware of any circumstance that may delay any Delivery Date, together with a recovery plan. Such notice shall not relieve Contractor of its obligations under this Article.

## ARTICLE 12 — DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

12.1 **HIPAA.** Contractor shall comply with the BAA attached as Exhibit D, which is incorporated herein by reference. In the event of conflict between this Agreement and the BAA with respect to PHI, the BAA shall control.
12.2 **Security Program.** Contractor shall maintain a written information security program consistent with NIST SP 800-53 moderate baseline, including encryption of PHI at rest and in transit, multi-factor authentication, and logging and monitoring of access.
12.3 **Security Incidents.** Contractor shall notify Client of any actual or reasonably suspected security incident affecting Client data within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery and shall bear all costs of investigation, notification, credit monitoring and remediation.
12.4 **Offshore Restrictions.** No PHI shall be accessed, stored or processed outside the United States without Client's prior written consent. Any Contractor Personnel located outside the United States shall access only de-identified data within Client-controlled environments.
12.5 **Audit.** Client or its designee may audit Contractor's compliance with this Article upon ten (10) days' notice, no more than once annually except following a security incident.

## ARTICLE 13 — INSURANCE

13.1 Contractor shall maintain, at its own expense, with carriers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability of $2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) professional liability/errors and omissions of $5,000,000 per claim; (c) cyber liability of $5,000,000 per claim; (d) workers' compensation as required by law; and (e) umbrella liability of $5,000,000.
13.2 Client shall be named as additional insured on the CGL and umbrella policies. Contractor shall furnish certificates of insurance prior to commencing the Services.

14.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
14.2 **Escalation.** The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through escalation to their respective executive sponsors for a period of thirty (30) days.
14.3 **Venue.** Any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Harris County, Texas, and each Party irrevocably submits to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.
14.4 **Jury Waiver.** EACH PARTY HEREBY WAIVES ITS RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.
