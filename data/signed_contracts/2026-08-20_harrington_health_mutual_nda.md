# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**Harrington Health Systems, Inc. — NDA No. HHS-NDA-2026-0412**

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 20, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Harrington Health Systems, Inc.**, a Texas nonprofit corporation with its principal place of business at 6400 Fannin Street, Suite 1800, Houston, Texas 77030 ("**Harrington**"), and **AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**AtliQ**"). Harrington and AtliQ are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 The Parties wish to evaluate a potential engagement under which AtliQ would design and implement an enterprise clinical and operational analytics platform for Harrington (the "**Purpose**"), and each Party expects to disclose Confidential Information to the other in connection with the Purpose.

## 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by a Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including system architecture, security documentation, pricing, proposals, methodologies, source code and business plans.

2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was lawfully known to it before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

2.3 **Protected Health Information.** Protected Health Information, as defined at 45 C.F.R. § 160.103, is excluded from this Agreement and shall be disclosed only after execution of a Business Associate Agreement between the Parties. Harrington shall not disclose, and AtliQ shall not request, Protected Health Information under this Agreement.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS

3.1 Each Receiving Party shall (a) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement; (c) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of like kind, and no less than reasonable care; and (d) promptly notify the Disclosing Party of any unauthorized use or disclosure of which it becomes aware.

3.2 Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by persons to whom it discloses Confidential Information.

3.3 A Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, provided it gives the Disclosing Party prompt prior written notice (where lawful) and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order, and discloses only the portion legally required.

## 4. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

4.1 Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information and confirm destruction in writing, except for copies retained in automatic backup systems or as required by law, which remain subject to this Agreement.

## 5. TERM

5.1 This Agreement applies to disclosures made during the two (2) years following the Effective Date. Either Party may terminate it earlier on thirty (30) days' written notice.

5.2 Each Party's obligations under Sections 3 and 4 continue for three (3) years after the end of the disclosure period, and, with respect to trade secrets, for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 6. NO LICENSE; NO WARRANTY; NO OBLIGATION

6.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No license is granted except as expressly stated.

6.2 Confidential Information is provided "AS IS." Neither Party is obligated to proceed with any transaction by reason of this Agreement.

## 7. REMEDIES

7.1 Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm for which damages would be an inadequate remedy, and that the Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy, without posting bond.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 **Governing Law; Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Any action arising out of this Agreement shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in Harris County, Texas.

8.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business.

8.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties.

8.4 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
