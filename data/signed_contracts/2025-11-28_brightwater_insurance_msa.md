# AGREEMENT FOR THE PROVISION OF TECHNOLOGY SERVICES

**Contract Reference:** BWG-US-TS-2025-1187
**Date of Agreement:** 28 November 2025

## 1. PARTIES

(1) **BRIGHTWATER INSURANCE INC.**, a corporation organised under the laws of the State of Connecticut, whose principal office is at 185 Asylum Street, 18th Floor, Hartford, Connecticut 06103, United States, a wholly owned subsidiary of Brightwater Group plc (the "**Customer**"); and

(2) **ATLIQ INC.**, a corporation organised under the laws of the State of Delaware (EIN 88-4410273), whose office is at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540, United States (the "**Supplier**").

## 2. BACKGROUND

(A) The Customer wishes to procure the development of a claims analytics data mart and associated reporting to support its property and casualty claims operations.

(B) The Supplier has agreed to provide such services on these terms, which are the standard technology services terms of the Brightwater Group.

## 3. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

3.1 In this Agreement:

"**Brightwater Group**" means Brightwater Group plc and each of its subsidiaries from time to time;

"**Charges**" means the charges payable for the Services as set out in Schedule 2;

"**Contract Value**" means the total Charges payable under this Agreement, being USD 92,000;

"**Customer Data**" means all data (including personal data) supplied by or on behalf of the Customer or any member of the Brightwater Group;

"**Deliverables**" means all outputs of the Services, including software, data models, reports and documentation;

"**Good Industry Practice**" means the exercise of the degree of skill, care, prudence and foresight which would reasonably be expected from a leading supplier of similar services;

"**Key Dates**" means the dates for delivery set out in Schedule 1;

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule 1;

"**Working Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or bank holiday in England or a federal holiday in the United States.

3.2 Headings do not affect interpretation. Words such as "including" shall be construed without limitation.

## 4. COMMENCEMENT AND DURATION

4.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date of this Agreement and shall continue for a period of eighteen (18) months unless terminated earlier in accordance with clause 16.

## 5. SUPPLY OF SERVICES

5.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services and Deliverables in accordance with this Agreement, Good Industry Practice, all applicable laws and the Brightwater Group policies notified to the Supplier.

5.2 The Supplier shall meet each Key Date. Time shall be of the essence in respect of the Key Dates.

5.3 The Supplier shall appoint a named account manager and shall provide monthly written progress reports to the Customer.

5.4 The Customer's representative shall be Mark Delaney, Chief Information Officer, or such other person as the Customer notifies.

## 6. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES

6.1 The Customer shall use reasonable endeavours to provide the Supplier with such information and access to Customer systems as the Supplier reasonably requests for the performance of the Services.

## 7. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

7.1 In consideration of the provision of the Services, the Customer shall pay the Charges in accordance with Schedule 2.

7.2 The Supplier shall invoice on the Customer's acceptance of each milestone, quoting the Customer's purchase order number. Invoices not quoting a valid purchase order number may be rejected.

7.3 The Customer shall pay correctly rendered and undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

7.4 The Customer may set off against any sums owed to the Supplier any amount owed by the Supplier to the Customer or any member of the Brightwater Group.

## 8. ACCEPTANCE

8.1 Each Deliverable shall be subject to acceptance tests determined by the Customer. The Customer shall notify the Supplier of acceptance or rejection within fifteen (15) Working Days of delivery.

8.2 If a Deliverable fails its acceptance tests, the Supplier shall remedy the failure and resubmit within ten (10) Working Days, at no additional charge.

## 9. DELAY AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

9.1 If the Supplier fails to achieve any Key Date, the Supplier shall pay to the Customer liquidated damages in the amount of one per cent (1%) of the Contract Value for each day or part day by which the relevant Key Date is missed.

9.2 Liquidated damages under clause 9.1 shall be payable in addition to any other right or remedy available to the Customer under this Agreement or at law, and the Customer may deduct such liquidated damages from any sums due to the Supplier.

9.3 The Supplier acknowledges that the liquidated damages set out in this clause 9 are proportionate to the Customer's legitimate interest in the timely delivery of the Services.

## 10. PERSONNEL

10.1 The Supplier shall ensure that all personnel engaged in the Services are suitably skilled, have passed background checks in accordance with the Brightwater Group Vendor Screening Standard, and comply with the Customer's security policies.

10.2 The Customer may require the removal of any Supplier personnel whose conduct is, in the Customer's reasonable opinion, unsatisfactory.

## 11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

11.1 All intellectual property rights in the Deliverables shall vest in the Customer on creation. The Supplier hereby assigns all such rights to the Customer with full title guarantee.

11.2 The Supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing materials and grants the Customer and the Brightwater Group a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use any such materials incorporated in the Deliverables.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1 The Supplier shall keep confidential all Customer Data and all information relating to the business of the Brightwater Group and shall not use it except for the performance of the Services.

12.2 The Customer may disclose the Supplier's information to any member of the Brightwater Group, its regulators and its professional advisers.

12.3 This clause 12 shall survive termination indefinitely.

## 13. DATA PROTECTION AND INFORMATION SECURITY

13.1 The Supplier shall comply with all applicable data protection laws, including the Connecticut Data Privacy Act and the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, and the Brightwater Group Information Security Schedule.

13.2 The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any security incident affecting Customer Data within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it.

13.3 The Supplier shall not process Customer Data outside the United States without the Customer's prior written consent.

## 14. WARRANTIES

14.1 The Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed in accordance with Good Industry Practice and that the Deliverables will be free from material defects for a period of twelve (12) months after acceptance.

## 15. INDEMNITIES AND LIABILITY

15.1 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer and each member of the Brightwater Group against all losses arising from (a) any breach by the Supplier of clauses 12 or 13, (b) any claim that the Deliverables infringe third-party rights, and (c) the negligence or wilful default of the Supplier.

15.2 Neither party shall be liable for any indirect or consequential loss.

15.3 Subject to clauses 9, 15.1 and 15.4, the Supplier's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed one hundred and fifty per cent (150%) of the Contract Value.

15.4 Nothing in this Agreement shall limit liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or any other liability which cannot be limited by law.

## 16. TERMINATION

16.1 The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.

16.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the other party commits a material breach which is not remedied within twenty (20) Working Days of notice.

16.3 On termination, the Supplier shall deliver all work in progress and Customer Data to the Customer.

## 17. INSURANCE

17.1 The Supplier shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of not less than USD 2,000,000 and cyber liability insurance of not less than USD 1,000,000.

## 18. AUDIT

18.1 The Supplier shall permit the Customer, the Brightwater Group internal audit function and any regulator to audit its compliance with this Agreement on reasonable notice.

## 19. GENERAL

19.1 The Supplier shall not assign or subcontract without the Customer's prior written consent. The Customer may assign this Agreement to any member of the Brightwater Group.

19.2 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. The Supplier's standard terms shall not apply.

19.3 No variation shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the parties.

19.4 A person who is not a party shall have no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any term, save that each member of the Brightwater Group may enforce clauses 12, 13 and 15.

## 20. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

20.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim (including non-contractual disputes or claims) arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales.

20.2 Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of London, England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
