# BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

**CareBridge Clinics, Inc. — BAA No. CBC-BAA-2025-014**

This Business Associate Agreement (this "**BAA**") is entered into as of October 6, 2025 (the "**BAA Effective Date**") by and between **CareBridge Clinics, Inc.**, an Arizona corporation with its principal place of business at 2200 North Central Avenue, Suite 900, Phoenix, Arizona 85004 ("**Covered Entity**"), and **AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Business Associate**"). Covered Entity and Business Associate are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Covered Entity and Business Associate are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated as of October 6, 2025 (together with its Statements of Work, the "**Master Services Agreement**"), under which Business Associate provides patient-scheduling analytics services to Covered Entity (the "**Services**").

B. In performing the Services, Business Associate may create, receive, maintain or transmit Protected Health Information on behalf of Covered Entity, and the Parties wish to comply with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act, and their implementing regulations at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164 (collectively, "**HIPAA**").

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this BAA have the meanings given in HIPAA, including "Breach," "Data Aggregation," "Designated Record Set," "Individual," "Required By Law," "Secretary," "Security Incident," "Subcontractor" and "Unsecured Protected Health Information."

1.2 "**Protected Health Information**" or "**PHI**" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103, limited to information created, received, maintained or transmitted by Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity.

1.3 "**De-identified Data**" means health information that has been de-identified in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.514(b) and that is therefore not PHI.

1.4 "**Covered Entity Environment**" means the cloud tenant, virtual desktops and other systems owned or controlled by Covered Entity and hosted in the United States, to which Covered Entity grants Business Associate access for the Services.

## 2. PERMITTED USES AND DISCLOSURES

2.1 **Services.** Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the Services under the Master Services Agreement, and as otherwise permitted or required by this BAA or as Required By Law.

2.2 **Minimum Necessary.** Business Associate shall limit its uses, disclosures and requests of PHI to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose, in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.502(b).

2.3 **Management and Administration.** Business Associate may use PHI for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities, and may disclose PHI for those purposes if the disclosure is Required By Law, or Business Associate obtains reasonable written assurances from the recipient that the PHI will be held confidentially, used or further disclosed only as Required By Law or for the purpose for which it was disclosed, and that the recipient will notify Business Associate of any instance in which the confidentiality of the PHI has been breached.

2.4 **De-identification.** Business Associate may de-identify PHI in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.514(b) solely for the purpose of performing the Services. De-identified Data may be used only for the Services and not for any other client, product or purpose.

2.5 **Data Aggregation.** Business Associate shall not perform Data Aggregation services unless expressly requested in writing by Covered Entity.

2.6 **No Sale; No Marketing.** Business Associate shall not sell PHI or use or disclose PHI for marketing purposes.

2.7 **Prohibited Uses.** Business Associate shall not use or disclose PHI in any manner that would violate Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 if done by Covered Entity.

## 3. LOCATION OF PHI AND OFFSHORE ACCESS

3.1 Business Associate shall access, store and process PHI only within the Covered Entity Environment, and only through personnel of Business Associate who are located in the United States and who have been identified to Covered Entity by name in writing.

3.2 Personnel of Business Associate or of any Subcontractor located outside the United States may access De-identified Data only, and only within the Covered Entity Environment or a secure environment approved in writing by Covered Entity. No PHI shall be accessed, stored or processed outside the United States.

3.3 Business Associate shall maintain, and provide to Covered Entity on request, a current list of personnel with access to PHI.

## 4. SAFEGUARDS

4.1 Business Associate shall use appropriate administrative, physical and technical safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 with respect to electronic PHI, to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as provided by this BAA.

4.2 Without limiting Section 4.1, Business Associate shall (a) encrypt electronic PHI in transit and at rest consistent with guidance issued by the Secretary; (b) require multi-factor authentication for access to PHI; (c) maintain audit logs of access to PHI; and (d) provide HIPAA privacy and security training to its personnel with access to PHI before access is granted and annually thereafter.

## 5. REPORTING

5.1 **Breaches and Non-Permitted Uses.** Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this BAA, including any Breach of Unsecured PHI, without unreasonable delay and in no case later than five (5) business days after discovery. A Breach shall be treated as discovered as of the first day on which it is known, or by exercising reasonable diligence would have been known, to Business Associate.

5.2 **Content.** The report shall include, to the extent then known, the identity of each Individual affected, a description of the incident, the PHI involved, and the steps taken to mitigate it. Business Associate shall supplement the report as further information becomes available.

5.3 **Security Incidents.** Business Associate shall report successful Security Incidents in accordance with Section 5.1. The Parties agree that this Section constitutes notice of the ongoing existence of unsuccessful Security Incidents (such as pings, port scans and blocked log-in attempts) for which no further notice is required.

5.4 **Mitigation and Notification.** Business Associate shall mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect of a use or disclosure in violation of this BAA. Covered Entity shall determine whether notification to Individuals, the Secretary or the media is required and shall control the content of any such notification. Business Associate shall reimburse Covered Entity's reasonable, documented costs of notification and credit monitoring to the extent the Breach was caused by Business Associate or its Subcontractors, subject to Section 9.

## 6. SUBCONTRACTORS

6.1 Business Associate shall ensure, in accordance with 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on behalf of Business Associate agrees in a written business associate agreement to the same restrictions, conditions and requirements that apply to Business Associate under this BAA, including Section 3.

6.2 Business Associate shall notify Covered Entity in writing of any Subcontractor engaged in connection with the Services and shall remain responsible for the performance of its Subcontractors.

## 7. INDIVIDUAL RIGHTS

7.1 **Access.** Within ten (10) business days of a request by Covered Entity, Business Associate shall make PHI in a Designated Record Set available to Covered Entity as necessary for Covered Entity to meet its obligations under 45 C.F.R. § 164.524.

7.2 **Amendment.** Within ten (10) business days of a request by Covered Entity, Business Associate shall make any amendment to PHI in a Designated Record Set as directed by Covered Entity under 45 C.F.R. § 164.526.

7.3 **Accounting.** Business Associate shall document disclosures of PHI and provide to Covered Entity, within fifteen (15) business days of request, the information required for Covered Entity to respond to a request for an accounting under 45 C.F.R. § 164.528.

7.4 Business Associate shall forward to Covered Entity within five (5) business days any request it receives directly from an Individual under this Section 7.

## 8. OTHER OBLIGATIONS

8.1 **Books and Records.** Business Associate shall make its internal practices, books and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary for purposes of determining compliance with HIPAA.

8.2 **Covered Entity Obligations.** To the extent Business Associate carries out an obligation of Covered Entity under Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164, Business Associate shall comply with the requirements of Subpart E that apply to Covered Entity in the performance of that obligation.

8.3 **Covered Entity Responsibilities.** Covered Entity shall (a) notify Business Associate of any limitation in its notice of privacy practices, any change in or revocation of an Individual's permission, and any restriction agreed under 45 C.F.R. § 164.522, to the extent it affects Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI; (b) disclose to Business Associate only the minimum PHI necessary for the Services; and (c) not request Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any manner not permissible under HIPAA.

8.4 **Assessment.** Upon thirty (30) days' written notice, and not more than once in any twelve (12) month period unless following a Breach, Covered Entity may request reasonable documentation (including a completed security questionnaire and relevant policies) to verify Business Associate's compliance with this BAA.

## 9. LIABILITY

9.1 Each Party's liability arising out of or relating to this BAA, including any Breach, is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability in the Master Services Agreement, under which aggregate liability for claims relating to a Breach or a violation of this BAA shall not exceed two (2) times the Fees paid or payable under the Master Services Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 10. TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1 **Term.** This BAA commences on the BAA Effective Date and continues until the Master Services Agreement expires or is terminated, subject to Section 10.4.

10.2 **Termination for Cause.** If either Party determines that the other has materially breached this BAA, it shall give written notice and a thirty (30) day period to cure. If the breach is not cured within that period, the non-breaching Party may terminate this BAA and the affected portions of the Master Services Agreement.

10.3 **Return or Destruction.** On termination or expiry, Business Associate shall, within thirty (30) days, return to Covered Entity or destroy all PHI in its possession or in the possession of its Subcontractors, and certify the destruction in writing. If return or destruction is infeasible, Business Associate shall notify Covered Entity of the conditions that make it infeasible, extend the protections of this BAA to that PHI, and limit further uses and disclosures to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, for so long as Business Associate maintains the PHI.

10.4 **Survival.** Section 10.3 and the obligations of Business Associate with respect to any PHI retained under it survive termination.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 **Amendment.** The Parties shall amend this BAA as necessary to comply with any change in HIPAA. No other amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

11.2 **Interpretation.** Any ambiguity in this BAA shall be resolved to permit compliance with HIPAA. In the event of conflict between this BAA and the Master Services Agreement with respect to PHI, this BAA controls; in all other respects the Master Services Agreement controls.

11.3 **No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this BAA confers any rights on any person other than the Parties.

11.4 **Governing Law.** This BAA is governed by the laws of the State of Arizona, except to the extent preempted by federal law, and disputes shall be resolved as provided in the Master Services Agreement.

11.5 **Counterparts.** This BAA may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
