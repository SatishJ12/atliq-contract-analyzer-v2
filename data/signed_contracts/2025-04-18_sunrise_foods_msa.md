# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made and entered into at Pune on 18 April 2025 (the "**Effective Date**")

**BETWEEN**

**SUNRISE FOODS PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at Sunrise House, NH 66 Bypass, Edappally, Kochi 682024, Kerala, India ("**Sunrise**" or the "**Client**");

**AND**

**ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456; GSTIN 27AAKCA4821F1Z3), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India ("**AtliQ**" or the "**Service Provider**").

## RECITALS

A. Sunrise manufactures and distributes packaged food products through a network of distributors across India and wishes to engage a technology partner to build and support digital tools for its distribution network.

B. AtliQ provides custom software development, data platform and analytics services.

C. The Parties intend that this Agreement shall govern all Statements of Work entered into between them during its term.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Sunday or a public holiday in Kerala or Maharashtra.

1.2 "**Deliverables**" means all software, code, designs, documentation and other outputs to be delivered under an SOW.

1.3 "**Monthly Support Fee**" means the monthly fee for support and maintenance services specified in an SOW.

1.4 "**Service Levels**" means the availability, response and resolution targets set out in Schedule A or the relevant SOW.

1.5 "**Service Credit**" means a credit against the Monthly Support Fee calculated in accordance with Clause 8.

1.6 "**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by authorised signatories of both Parties that references this Agreement. SOW-1 (Distributor Portal v1) is executed concurrently with this Agreement.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 AtliQ shall provide the services described in each SOW (the "**Services**") in accordance with this Agreement.

2.2 SOW-1 covers the design, development and deployment of a web-based distributor portal (order placement, invoice and ledger view, scheme and promotion visibility) ("**Distributor Portal v1**"), together with post-go-live support and maintenance for twelve (12) months.

2.3 Sunrise's project owner shall be Ms. Meera Pillai. AtliQ's project owner shall be designated in each SOW.

2.4 Each SOW shall be a separate contract incorporating the terms of this Agreement. In the event of conflict, this Agreement prevails unless the SOW expressly states otherwise.

## 3. CHANGE MANAGEMENT

3.1 Changes to scope, timelines or fees shall be agreed through a written change request signed by both Parties' project owners.

## 4. OBLIGATIONS OF SUNRISE

4.1 Sunrise shall provide timely access to its ERP (including test environments), master data, business users and approvals required for the Services.

4.2 Sunrise shall be responsible for the accuracy of data it provides.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 The total Fees under SOW-1 are **USD 33,000** (United States Dollars Thirty-Three Thousand only), comprising build fees payable on Milestones and a Monthly Support Fee for the support period, as detailed in SOW-1.

5.2 AtliQ shall invoice build fees upon completion of each Milestone and the Monthly Support Fee monthly in arrears. Sunrise shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

5.3 All amounts are exclusive of GST, which shall be charged additionally at the applicable rate. Sunrise may deduct TDS as required by law.

5.4 Delayed payments shall attract interest at twelve percent (12%) per annum.

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 This Agreement shall be valid for a period of three (3) years from the Effective Date, i.e., until 17 April 2028, and may be renewed by mutual written agreement.

6.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.

6.3 Sunrise may terminate any SOW for convenience by giving forty-five (45) days' written notice, subject to payment for all work performed up to the effective date of termination.

6.4 Expiry or termination of this Agreement shall not affect any SOW then in force, which shall continue to be governed by this Agreement until its completion.

## 7. ACCEPTANCE

7.1 Sunrise shall conduct user acceptance testing within ten (10) Business Days of each delivery. Deliverables shall be deemed accepted upon written sign-off, or if no material defects are reported within such period, or upon use in production.

## 8. SERVICE LEVELS AND SERVICE CREDITS

8.1 During the support period under any SOW, AtliQ shall meet the Service Levels set out in Schedule A.

8.2 If AtliQ fails to meet a Service Level in any calendar month for reasons attributable to AtliQ, Sunrise shall be entitled to Service Credits as set out in Schedule A.

8.3 The total Service Credits in any calendar month shall not exceed five percent (5%) of the Monthly Support Fee for that month.

8.4 Service Credits shall be applied against the next invoice and shall be Sunrise's sole monetary remedy for failure to meet the Service Levels. Service Credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services and are not a penalty.

8.5 No Service Credit shall accrue for failures caused by Sunrise, its third-party vendors, scheduled maintenance notified in advance, or Force Majeure.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Each Party shall treat the other Party's confidential information (including distributor lists, pricing, schemes and source code) as strictly confidential and use it only for performing this Agreement.

9.2 This obligation shall survive for three (3) years after termination.

## 10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

10.1 Upon payment in full, Sunrise shall own all intellectual property rights in the Deliverables developed specifically for Sunrise under an SOW.

10.2 AtliQ retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, components, libraries and know-how. AtliQ grants Sunrise a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use any such pre-existing materials to the extent incorporated in the Deliverables.

## 11. WARRANTIES

11.1 AtliQ warrants that the Services will be performed with due skill and care, and that Deliverables will materially conform to the specifications for ninety (90) days post go-live.

## 12. INDEMNITY

12.1 Each Party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims arising out of its breach of confidentiality, infringement of intellectual property rights, or gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

## 13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

13.1 Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, consequential or special losses or loss of profits.

13.2 Each Party's aggregate liability under an SOW shall not exceed the Fees paid or payable under that SOW.

## 14. DATA PROTECTION

14.1 AtliQ shall comply with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the rules thereunder, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 to the extent applicable, in relation to any personal data of distributors or employees processed in performing the Services.

## 15. NON-SOLICITATION

15.1 Neither Party shall solicit the employees of the other Party engaged in the Services during the term of the relevant SOW and six (6) months thereafter.

## 16. FORCE MAJEURE

16.1 Neither Party shall be liable for failure or delay due to causes beyond its reasonable control.

## 17. GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION

17.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

17.2 Any dispute not resolved amicably within thirty (30) days shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed mutually under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Pune, Maharashtra, and the proceedings shall be in English.

17.3 The courts at Pune shall have exclusive jurisdiction, subject to Clause 17.2.

## 18. GENERAL

18.1 Neither Party shall assign this Agreement without prior written consent of the other.

18.2 This Agreement and its SOWs constitute the entire agreement. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

18.3 Notices shall be sent to the registered addresses above and by email to the project owners.
