# PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Crestline Contract No.:** CRI-VND-24-0318
**Effective Date:** August 1, 2024

This Professional Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

**CRESTLINE RETAIL INC.**, an Ohio corporation with its principal place of business at 250 West Nationwide Boulevard, Suite 900, Columbus, Ohio 43215 ("**Crestline**"); and

**ATLIQ INC.**, a Delaware corporation (EIN 88-4410273) with its office at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Supplier**").

Crestline and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and collectively the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Crestline operates retail stores and e-commerce channels in the United States and requires data engineering and business intelligence resources on a time-and-materials basis; and

WHEREAS, Supplier is in the business of providing such resources and wishes to provide them to Crestline on the terms set out below.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity or the power to direct its management.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by a Party that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

1.3 "**Deliverables**" means all work product created by Supplier Personnel specifically for Crestline under a Work Order.

1.4 "**Rate Card**" means the hourly rates set out in Exhibit B.

1.5 "**Supplier Personnel**" means employees and approved subcontractors of Supplier assigned to perform Services.

1.6 "**Work Order**" means an order for Services substantially in the form of Exhibit A, executed by both Parties.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 Supplier shall provide the data engineering, business intelligence and analytics services described in each Work Order (the "**Services**").

2.2 Each Work Order shall specify the roles, estimated hours, duration, and any Deliverables. Each Work Order is governed by this Agreement.

2.3 Crestline's primary contact for this Agreement is Laura Brennan, VP Data.

## 3. SUPPLIER PERSONNEL

3.1 Supplier shall assign qualified Supplier Personnel with skills commensurate with the roles in the Rate Card. Crestline may request the replacement of any Supplier Personnel on reasonable grounds on ten (10) business days' notice.

3.2 Supplier Personnel shall comply with Crestline's information security, acceptable use and on-site policies.

3.3 Supplier is an independent contractor and is solely responsible for the compensation, benefits and taxes of Supplier Personnel.

## 4. TERM

4.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years, until July 31, 2027, unless earlier terminated under Section 11.

4.2 Work Orders in effect at expiry shall continue to be governed by this Agreement until completed or terminated.

## 5. TIMEKEEPING AND INVOICING

5.1 Supplier Personnel shall record time in Crestline's designated timekeeping system, and time shall be approved weekly by the Crestline Work Order manager.

5.2 Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears for approved hours at the applicable Rate Card rates.

5.3 Crestline shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.

5.4 Travel expenses are reimbursable only if pre-approved in writing and incurred in accordance with Crestline's travel policy.

## 6. RATES

6.1 The rates for Services are set out in the Rate Card (Exhibit B).

6.2 The Rate Card rates are fixed for the first twelve (12) months of the Term. Thereafter, Supplier may increase rates once per contract year by no more than three percent (3%) upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

6.3 Rates are inclusive of all Supplier overheads, equipment and administrative costs.

6.4 Supplier represents and warrants that the rates, fees and other commercial terms offered to Crestline under this Agreement are and shall remain no less favorable than those offered by Supplier or its Affiliates to any other customer for substantially similar services during the Term. If, during the Term, Supplier or any of its Affiliates offers lower rates to any other customer for substantially similar services, Supplier shall promptly notify Crestline, and such lower rates shall apply to Crestline retroactively from the date they were first offered to such other customer. Supplier shall issue a credit to Crestline for the difference between the amounts invoiced and the amounts that would have been invoiced at such lower rates, within thirty (30) days.

6.5 Upon reasonable notice and no more than once per year, Crestline or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality may audit Supplier's records to verify compliance with this Section 6.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in confidence, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to its personnel and advisors with a need to know who are bound by obligations at least as protective.

7.2 The obligations in Section 7.1 do not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available without breach, was known to the recipient prior to disclosure, is independently developed, or is rightfully received from a third party.

7.3 These obligations survive for three (3) years following termination, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 Upon payment, Crestline shall own all right, title and interest in the Deliverables.

8.2 Supplier retains all rights in its pre-existing materials, tools and know-how ("**Supplier Materials**"). To the extent Supplier Materials are incorporated in a Deliverable, Supplier grants Crestline a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use them as part of such Deliverable.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards.

9.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Crestline from third-party claims arising from (a) Supplier's gross negligence or willful misconduct, (b) infringement by the Deliverables of any third-party intellectual property right, or (c) any claim by Supplier Personnel for wages, benefits or employment status.

10.2 Crestline shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier from third-party claims arising from materials provided by Crestline or Crestline's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 11. TERMINATION

11.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Work Order for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.

11.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure within thirty (30) days.

11.3 Upon termination, Crestline shall pay for all approved hours performed through the effective date of termination.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 EXCEPT FOR BREACH OF SECTION 7 OR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

12.2 EXCEPT FOR BREACH OF SECTION 7 OR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CRESTLINE UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

## 13. DATA SECURITY

13.1 Supplier shall comply with Crestline's Vendor Data Security Requirements, as updated from time to time, and all applicable U.S. federal and state privacy laws, including the Ohio Data Protection Act where applicable.

13.2 Supplier shall notify Crestline within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of any security incident affecting Crestline data.

13.3 Supplier shall not access Crestline production data from outside the United States without Crestline's prior written approval.

## 14. INSURANCE

14.1 Supplier shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence, professional liability (errors and omissions) insurance of at least $2,000,000, and workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

## 15. NON-SOLICITATION

15.1 During the Term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, Crestline shall not directly solicit for employment any Supplier Personnel assigned to Crestline, save with Supplier's consent.

## 16. GOVERNING LAW; VENUE

16.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Ohio, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

16.2 The Parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Franklin County, Ohio.

## 17. MISCELLANEOUS

17.1 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all assets.

17.2 This Agreement, including its Exhibits and Work Orders, is the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior agreements relating to its subject matter.

17.3 Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties. Waiver of any breach is not a waiver of any other breach.

17.4 Notices shall be sent to: Crestline — Attn: VP Data, with copy to Legal Department, at the address above; Supplier — Attn: President, at the address above, with a copy to karandeep@atliq.com.

17.5 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and electronically.
