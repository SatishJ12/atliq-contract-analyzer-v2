# STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

**CloudSpan Agreement No.:** CSP-PTN-IN-2025-031
**Effective Date:** 1 September 2025

## PARTIES

This Strategic Partnership Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made between:

1. **CLOUDSPAN TECHNOLOGIES PTE. LTD.**, a company incorporated in Singapore (UEN 201834567K), having its registered office at 8 Marina View, #29-01 Asia Square Tower 1, Singapore 018960 ("**CloudSpan**"); and

2. **ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a private limited company incorporated under the laws of India (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456; GSTIN 27AAKCA4821F1Z3), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India ("**Partner**"),

each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## RECITALS

A. CloudSpan provides the CloudSpan Platform, a cloud lakehouse and data-warehouse platform offered as a service.

B. Partner provides software, data engineering, analytics, business intelligence and AI/ML services to enterprise customers.

C. The Parties wish to establish a strategic relationship under which Partner will implement the CloudSpan Platform for customers and refer prospective customers to CloudSpan, on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, in relation to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty per cent (50%) of the voting securities or the power to direct management.

1.2 "**CloudSpan Platform**" means CloudSpan's cloud lakehouse and data-warehouse service, together with associated tools, connectors and documentation made generally available by CloudSpan from time to time.

1.3 "**Customer**" means a third party that subscribes to the CloudSpan Platform directly from CloudSpan.

1.4 "**Referred Customer**" means a Customer introduced by Partner through a Referral Registration accepted by CloudSpan under Clause 4.

1.5 "**Referral Registration**" means a registration of a prospective customer submitted by Partner through the CloudSpan Partner Portal.

1.6 "**First-Year Subscription Fees**" means the subscription fees actually received by CloudSpan from a Referred Customer for the first twelve (12) months of its initial subscription, excluding taxes, professional services fees, usage overages billed separately and refunds or credits.

1.7 "**Certified Engineer**" means an employee of Partner who holds a current CloudSpan Certified Implementation Engineer credential.

1.8 "**Implementation Services**" means services provided by Partner to Customers for the design, migration, configuration, integration and deployment of solutions on the CloudSpan Platform.

1.9 "**Competing Platform**" has the meaning given in Clause 7.2.

1.10 "**Territory**" means the Republic of India and the member states of the Gulf Cooperation Council, being the United Arab Emirates, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, the State of Qatar, the State of Kuwait, the Kingdom of Bahrain and the Sultanate of Oman.

1.11 "**Marks**" means a Party's trade names, trade marks, logos and partner badges.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 CloudSpan appoints Partner as a Premier Implementation and Referral Partner for the CloudSpan Platform. Partner accepts the appointment.

2.2 Save as provided in Clause 7, the appointment is non-exclusive. CloudSpan may appoint other partners in any territory, and Partner may operate outside the Territory without restriction.

2.3 Partner is not appointed as a reseller. Customers contract with and pay CloudSpan directly for subscriptions to the CloudSpan Platform.

## 3. PARTNER COMMITMENTS

3.1 Partner shall maintain, throughout the Term, not fewer than four (4) Certified Engineers. Where the number falls below four (4) for reasons outside Partner's reasonable control, Partner shall have ninety (90) days to restore it.

3.2 Partner shall deliver Implementation Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with CloudSpan's published implementation guidelines, and shall be solely responsible to Customers for Implementation Services it contracts to provide.

3.3 Partner shall not make any representation or warranty concerning the CloudSpan Platform beyond those contained in CloudSpan's published documentation.

3.4 Partner shall participate in a joint business-planning review with CloudSpan at least once every six (6) months.

## 4. REFERRALS AND REFERRAL FEES

4.1 Partner may submit Referral Registrations for prospective customers. CloudSpan shall accept or reject each Referral Registration within ten (10) business days. A registration shall be rejected only where the prospect is an existing Customer or is already the subject of an active opportunity registered by CloudSpan or another partner in the preceding ninety (90) days.

4.2 An accepted Referral Registration shall remain valid for one hundred and eighty (180) days. If the prospect signs a subscription order with CloudSpan within that period, it becomes a Referred Customer.

4.3 CloudSpan shall pay Partner a referral fee equal to twelve per cent (12%) of the First-Year Subscription Fees received from each Referred Customer (the "**Referral Fee**").

4.4 CloudSpan shall provide Partner with a quarterly statement of Referral Fees within fifteen (15) days of the end of each calendar quarter. Partner shall invoice CloudSpan on the basis of that statement, and CloudSpan shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

4.5 Referral Fees are exclusive of GST and other applicable taxes. Where CloudSpan is required by law to withhold tax, it shall provide Partner with the relevant certificate to enable Partner to claim credit.

4.6 Partner may, on reasonable notice and not more than once per year, have CloudSpan's records relating to Referral Fees reviewed by an independent accountant, at Partner's cost, unless an underpayment of more than five per cent (5%) is found, in which case CloudSpan shall bear the cost.

## 5. CLOUDSPAN COMMITMENTS

5.1 CloudSpan shall provide Partner with:
   (a) twenty (20) complimentary certification vouchers per contract year;
   (b) a non-production sandbox environment of the CloudSpan Platform for training and demonstration;
   (c) access to the CloudSpan Partner Portal, partner-level technical support and early-access programmes; and
   (d) a named partner manager.

5.2 CloudSpan shall list Partner in its public partner directory for the Territory and shall use reasonable efforts to refer Implementation Services opportunities in the Territory to Partner.

5.3 The Parties shall agree an annual co-marketing plan. CloudSpan shall contribute market development funds of up to USD 25,000 per contract year for jointly approved activities.

## 6. MARKETING AND USE OF MARKS

6.1 Each Party grants the other a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free licence during the Term to use its Marks solely to identify the partnership, in accordance with the owning Party's brand guidelines.

6.2 Neither Party shall issue any press release concerning this Agreement without the prior written approval of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 7. EXCLUSIVITY

7.1 In consideration of the Referral Fees, market development funds and other benefits under this Agreement, during the Term Partner and its Affiliates shall not act as an implementation, reseller or referral partner for any Competing Platform in India or the GCC.

7.2 "**Competing Platform**" means any cloud data-warehouse, lakehouse or unified analytics platform offered as a service.

7.3 For clarity, Clause 7.1 does not restrict Partner or its Affiliates from (a) providing services outside India and the GCC; or (b) providing services to a customer that has independently selected a platform, provided Partner does not hold a partner appointment with, or receive any fee or benefit from, the provider of that platform.

7.4 In this Clause 7, "GCC" has the meaning given to the member states listed in the definition of Territory.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 Each Party retains all rights in its own intellectual property. CloudSpan owns the CloudSpan Platform and all improvements to it.

8.2 Partner retains all rights in its own methodologies, accelerators, tools, templates and know-how, whether developed before or during the Term, including those used in delivering Implementation Services.

8.3 Any feedback provided by Partner concerning the CloudSpan Platform may be used by CloudSpan without restriction, provided Partner's Confidential Information is not disclosed.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other in connection with this Agreement ("**Confidential Information**"), use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to its employees, Affiliates and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

9.2 Clause 9.1 does not apply to information that is public other than through breach, already lawfully known, lawfully received from a third party, or independently developed, or to disclosure required by law.

9.3 The obligations in this Clause 9 survive for three (3) years after termination or expiry.

## 10. DATA PROTECTION

10.1 Each Party shall comply with data protection laws applicable to it. Where Partner processes Customer personal data in providing Implementation Services, it does so under its own agreement with that Customer.

10.2 Referral Registrations shall contain only business contact information that Partner is entitled to share.

## 11. WARRANTIES

11.1 Each Party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and that doing so does not breach any other agreement binding on it.

11.2 Save as expressly stated, all warranties, conditions and representations, whether express or implied, are excluded to the extent permitted by law.

## 12. INDEMNITIES

12.1 CloudSpan shall indemnify Partner against third-party claims that the CloudSpan Platform infringes that third party's intellectual property rights.

12.2 Partner shall indemnify CloudSpan against third-party claims that Partner's own materials or Implementation Services infringe that third party's intellectual property rights.

12.3 The indemnified Party shall notify the other promptly of any claim, allow it conduct of the defence and provide reasonable assistance at its cost.

## 13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

13.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, consequential or special loss, or loss of profit, revenue or goodwill.

13.2 Each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement in any contract year shall not exceed the greater of (a) the total Referral Fees paid or payable in the preceding twelve (12) months and (b) USD 100,000.

13.3 Clauses 13.1 and 13.2 do not apply to liability under Clause 12, breach of Clause 9, or liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 14. TERM AND TERMINATION

14.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years, until 31 August 2028 (the "**Term**"), unless terminated earlier. The Parties may renew it by written agreement.

14.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving not less than ninety (90) days' written notice to the other.

14.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy, or that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice, or becomes insolvent.

14.4 On termination or expiry:
   (a) Referral Fees for Referred Customers whose subscriptions were signed before the termination date remain payable;
   (b) Clause 7 shall cease to apply from the date termination takes effect;
   (c) each Party shall stop using the other's Marks; and
   (d) Clauses 4.4 to 4.6, 8, 9, 12, 13, 15 and 16 survive.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

15.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Singapore.

15.2 The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussions between senior executives for thirty (30) days.

15.3 Any dispute not so resolved shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre ("**SIAC**") under the SIAC Rules in force at the time. The seat of arbitration shall be Singapore. The tribunal shall consist of one (1) arbitrator. The language of arbitration shall be English.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 **Relationship.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership in law, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

16.2 **Non-solicitation.** During the Term and for six (6) months thereafter, neither Party shall actively solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom it had material contact under this Agreement. General advertisements are not solicitation.

16.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except to an Affiliate or successor in a merger or sale of substantially all assets, on written notice.

16.4 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

16.5 **Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses above, with a copy by email: for CloudSpan to partnerships-legal@cloudspan.io (attention: Wei Lin Tan, Head of Partnerships); for Partner to karandeep@atliq.com.

16.6 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. It may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties.

16.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed electronically and in counterparts.
