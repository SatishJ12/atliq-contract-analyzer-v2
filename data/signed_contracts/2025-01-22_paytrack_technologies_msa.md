# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**PayTrack Ref:** PT/LEGAL/MSA/2025/004
**Execution Version — 22 January 2025**

This Master Services Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into on 22 January 2025 ("**Effective Date**") between:

**PAYTRACK TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 3rd Floor, Prestige Tech Vista, Outer Ring Road, Bellandur, Bengaluru 560103, Karnataka, India ("**PayTrack**" or "**Client**"); and

**ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456; GSTIN 27AAKCA4821F1Z3), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India ("**AtliQ**" or "**Vendor**").

## RECITALS

A. PayTrack operates a payments reconciliation and merchant settlement platform and wishes to build a transaction analytics and anomaly detection module.

B. AtliQ provides data engineering, machine learning and software development services.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which AtliQ will provide such services.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Applicable Law**" means all laws, regulations, and binding directions of any governmental or regulatory authority, including the Reserve Bank of India, applicable to a Party.

1.2 "**Client Data**" means all data, including transaction data and merchant data, provided by or on behalf of PayTrack.

1.3 "**Deliverables**" means all software, models, code, documentation and other materials delivered under an SOW.

1.4 "**Losses**" means all losses, damages, liabilities, costs (including reasonable legal fees), fines and penalties.

1.5 "**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by both Parties under this Agreement.

## 2. SCOPE

2.1 AtliQ shall perform the services set out in each SOW ("**Services**"). SOW-1 covers the design and development of a transaction analytics data platform and an ML-based anomaly detection module for merchant settlements.

2.2 PayTrack's relationship owner is Ms. Shreya Iyer.

2.3 AtliQ shall comply with PayTrack's information security policies and the RBI guidelines on outsourcing of IT services as communicated by PayTrack in writing.

## 3. CHANGE CONTROL

3.1 Any change to the scope, timeline or fees under an SOW shall be documented in a change order signed by both Parties.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The total fees under SOW-1 are **USD 71,000** (United States Dollars Seventy-One Thousand only), payable in accordance with the milestone schedule in SOW-1.

4.2 AtliQ shall invoice upon completion of each milestone. PayTrack shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

4.3 Fees are exclusive of GST. PayTrack may deduct TDS in accordance with Applicable Law.

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for two (2) years unless terminated earlier.

5.2 Either Party may terminate for material breach not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice.

5.3 PayTrack may terminate an SOW for convenience on thirty (30) days' notice, subject to payment for all Services performed and committed costs up to the termination date.

5.4 PayTrack may terminate this Agreement if directed to do so by a regulator, on such notice as the regulator requires.

## 6. ACCEPTANCE

6.1 PayTrack shall review each Deliverable within ten (10) business days. Deliverables shall be deemed accepted if no written rejection specifying material non-conformities is received within that period.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential the other Party's confidential information and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.

7.2 AtliQ acknowledges that Client Data may include payment system data subject to RBI data localisation requirements, and shall ensure that such data is stored only in India.

7.3 These obligations survive for five (5) years following termination, and indefinitely in respect of Client Data.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 Upon full payment, all intellectual property in the Deliverables developed specifically for PayTrack shall vest in PayTrack.

8.2 AtliQ retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, tools and know-how and grants PayTrack a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use such materials as incorporated in the Deliverables.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 AtliQ warrants that the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with Applicable Law, and that the Deliverables will materially conform to the SOW for ninety (90) days after acceptance.

9.2 PayTrack warrants that it has the necessary rights and consents to provide Client Data to AtliQ for the purposes of the Services.

## 10. INDEMNITY

10.1 Each Party (the "**Indemnifying Party**") shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other Party and its directors, officers and employees (the "**Indemnified Party**") from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent caused by: (a) the Indemnifying Party's breach of Clause 7 (Confidentiality) or Clause 12 (Data Protection); (b) the gross negligence, wilful misconduct or fraud of the Indemnifying Party or its personnel; or (c) in the case of AtliQ, any claim that the Deliverables infringe a third party's intellectual property rights, and in the case of PayTrack, any claim that Client Data or materials supplied by PayTrack infringe a third party's rights.

10.2 Neither Party shall be required to indemnify the other to the extent any Losses are caused by the negligence, breach or wilful misconduct of the Indemnified Party.

10.3 The Indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, permit the Indemnifying Party to control the defence and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the Indemnifying Party's cost.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, special, incidental or consequential damages, or loss of profits, revenue or goodwill.

11.2 Each Party's total aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under the SOW giving rise to the claim.

11.3 The limitations in this Clause 11 shall not apply to liability for fraud or wilful misconduct.

## 12. DATA PROTECTION

12.1 AtliQ shall process personal data comprised in Client Data only on PayTrack's documented instructions and in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable RBI directions.

12.2 AtliQ shall notify PayTrack of any data breach within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it, and shall cooperate with PayTrack in any reporting to CERT-In or the RBI.

12.3 AtliQ shall permit PayTrack and its regulators to audit its compliance with this Clause 12 on reasonable notice.

## 13. NON-SOLICITATION

13.1 Neither Party shall solicit for employment the other Party's employees engaged in the Services during the term and for twelve (12) months thereafter.

## 14. FORCE MAJEURE

14.1 Neither Party shall be liable for delay or non-performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control. AtliQ shall maintain a business continuity plan appropriate to the Services.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

15.2 Disputes not resolved by negotiation between senior management within thirty (30) days shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Pune, and the language shall be English.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other.

16.2 This Agreement, with its SOWs, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior drafts and negotiations.

16.3 Amendments must be in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

16.4 This Agreement may be executed electronically and in counterparts.
