## 1. ENGAGEMENT

1.1 AtliQ engages the Contractor as an independent contractor to provide the services described in the Engagement Schedule (the "**Services**"), and the Contractor accepts the engagement.

1.2 The Contractor shall perform the Services personally and shall not subcontract any part of them without AtliQ's prior written consent.

1.3 The Contractor may determine the manner and hours of work, subject to meeting the timelines and quality standards reasonably specified by AtliQ and attending agreed client and team meetings.

## 2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement commences on 28 April 2025 and continues for twelve (12) months, until 27 April 2026, unless terminated earlier or extended in writing.

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 AtliQ shall pay the Contractor the fees set out in the Engagement Schedule.

3.2 The Contractor shall submit a monthly invoice, supported by a timesheet approved by the AtliQ project lead. AtliQ shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

3.3 AtliQ shall deduct tax at source under the Income Tax Act, 1961 as applicable and provide TDS certificates. The Contractor is responsible for his own GST registration (if required) and all other taxes.

3.4 AtliQ shall reimburse reasonable pre-approved travel expenses incurred for client visits.

## 4. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

4.1 The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner or agent of AtliQ. The Contractor is not entitled to salary, provident fund, gratuity, leave or other employee benefits.

4.2 The Contractor may provide services to other persons during the Term, provided this does not affect performance of the Services or breach Clause 6.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 In this Clause, "**Work Product**" means all dashboards, reports, data models, DAX measures, Power Query scripts, visual templates, documentation and other materials created by the Contractor in performing the Services.

5.2 The Contractor retains ownership of all intellectual property rights in the Work Product and in any pre-existing materials, components and templates of the Contractor.

5.3 The Contractor grants AtliQ a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide licence to use, copy, modify and deploy the Work Product for the purposes of AtliQ's business, including for delivery to AtliQ's clients.

5.4 The Contractor shall not incorporate into the Work Product any third-party material that AtliQ is not entitled to use under Clause 5.3.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other party, and in the case of AtliQ, of AtliQ's clients, received in connection with this Agreement, and shall use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.

6.2 The Contractor shall comply with any confidentiality and data-security requirements of AtliQ's clients notified to him in writing, including restrictions on downloading client data to personal devices.

6.3 This Clause 6 does not apply to information that is public, already lawfully known, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

6.4 The obligations in this Clause 6 survive for three (3) years after termination or expiry.

## 7. WARRANTIES

7.1 The Contractor warrants that he has the skills and experience to perform the Services and will perform them with reasonable skill and care.

7.2 The Contractor warrants that the Work Product will not, to his knowledge, infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party.

## 8. LIABILITY

8.1 Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable to the Contractor in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, except for breach of Clause 6 or fraud.

8.2 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

## 9. TERMINATION

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving fifteen (15) days' written notice.

9.2 Either party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach not remedied within seven (7) days of notice.

9.3 On termination, AtliQ shall pay for Services performed up to the termination date, and the Contractor shall hand over all Work Product in progress and return AtliQ and client materials.

9.4 Clauses 5, 6, 8 and 10 survive termination or expiry.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Non-solicitation.** During the Term and for six (6) months thereafter, the Contractor shall not directly solicit any AtliQ client to whom he was introduced through AtliQ for the purpose of providing the same Services to that client independently of AtliQ.

10.2 **Assignment.** The Contractor may not assign this Agreement. AtliQ may assign it to an Affiliate on notice.

10.3 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, including the Engagement Schedule, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter.

10.4 **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Pune shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

10.5 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed electronically.
