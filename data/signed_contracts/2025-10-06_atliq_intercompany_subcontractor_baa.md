# SUBCONTRACTOR BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

**Intercompany — Ref. ATQ-IC-BAA-2025-01**

This Subcontractor Business Associate Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is made as of October 6, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") between:

**AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation (EIN 88-4410273) with its principal place of business at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540, USA ("**Business Associate**"); and

**AtliQ Technologies Pvt Ltd**, a private limited company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India ("**Subcontractor**"),

each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## RECITALS

A. Business Associate has entered into a Business Associate Agreement with the Covered Entity identified in Schedule 1 (the "**Upstream BAA**").

B. Subcontractor provides software development and data engineering services to Business Associate under the intercompany services agreement between the Parties (the "**Intercompany Agreement**").

C. The Parties enter into this Agreement to satisfy 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2) in respect of the services Subcontractor performs for the Engagement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in HIPAA (45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164) or, if not defined there, in the Upstream BAA.

1.2 "**Engagement**" means the CareBridge Engagement described in Schedule 1.

1.3 "**Engagement Services**" means the services performed by Subcontractor for Business Associate under the Intercompany Agreement in connection with the Engagement.

1.4 "**De-identified Data**" means data de-identified in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.514(b) by Business Associate or the Covered Entity before it is made available to Subcontractor.

## 2. SCOPE

2.1 This Agreement applies to the Engagement Services. Any services Subcontractor performs for Business Associate other than the Engagement Services are governed by the Intercompany Agreement.

2.2 The restrictions, conditions and requirements of the Upstream BAA that apply to Business Associate apply equally to Subcontractor in performing the Engagement Services.

## 3. PERMITTED ACCESS

3.1 Subcontractor and its personnel shall access De-identified Data only. Subcontractor shall not access, receive, store, view or process PHI.

3.2 Subcontractor shall access De-identified Data only within the Covered Entity Environment (as defined in the Upstream BAA) or another secure environment approved in writing by Business Associate and the Covered Entity. Subcontractor shall not download, copy or transfer De-identified Data to its own systems or devices.

3.3 Subcontractor shall not attempt to re-identify any Individual or to link De-identified Data with any other data.

3.4 If Subcontractor becomes aware that any data made available to it contains PHI, Subcontractor shall immediately cease access and notify Business Associate within one (1) business day, and such event shall be handled as a use or disclosure not provided for by this Agreement under Section 5.

## 4. SAFEGUARDS

4.1 Subcontractor shall implement administrative, physical and technical safeguards appropriate to the data it accesses, including multi-factor authentication, role-based access limited to personnel listed by Business Associate, and HIPAA awareness training for all personnel before access is granted.

4.2 Subcontractor shall not engage any further subcontractor for the Engagement Services.

## 5. REPORTING

5.1 Subcontractor shall report to Business Associate any use or disclosure not provided for by this Agreement, any Security Incident and any actual or suspected Breach within two (2) business days after discovery, so as to allow Business Associate to meet its reporting obligations under the Upstream BAA.

5.2 Subcontractor shall cooperate with Business Associate and the Covered Entity in any investigation and mitigation.

## 6. RECORDS

6.1 Subcontractor shall make its internal practices, books and records relating to the Engagement Services available to Business Associate and to the Secretary for purposes of determining compliance with HIPAA.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 This Agreement continues until the Upstream BAA expires or is terminated, or until the Engagement Services end, whichever is earlier.

7.2 Business Associate may terminate this Agreement and the Engagement Services immediately on written notice if Subcontractor breaches Section 3.

7.3 On termination, Subcontractor shall cease all access to data relating to the Engagement and shall confirm in writing that it holds no copies.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, except to the extent preempted by federal law.

8.2 In the event of conflict between this Agreement and the Intercompany Agreement with respect to the Engagement Services, this Agreement controls.

8.3 This Agreement may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties, and may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
