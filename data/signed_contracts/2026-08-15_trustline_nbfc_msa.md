# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into at Hyderabad on 15 August 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**TRUSTLINE NBFC LIMITED**, a non-banking financial company registered with the Reserve Bank of India and incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 8th Floor, Cyber Pearl, HITEC City, Madhapur, Hyderabad 500081, Telangana, India (the "**Company**"); and

**ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456; GSTIN 27AAKCA4821F1Z3), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India (the "**Service Provider**").

## RECITALS

A. The Company is engaged in the business of retail and MSME lending and wishes to develop a collections analytics and early-warning dashboard.

B. The Service Provider is engaged in data analytics, business intelligence and software development services.

C. The Company has conducted due diligence on the Service Provider in accordance with the RBI Master Direction on Outsourcing of Information Technology Services and the Parties wish to record the terms of the engagement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Company Data**" means all data, including borrower data, loan account data and collections data, provided by or on behalf of the Company.

1.2 "**Deliverables**" means the software, dashboards, models and documentation specified in the Statement of Work.

1.3 "**Milestone**" and "**Milestone Fee**" have the meanings given in the Statement of Work.

1.4 "**Losses**" means all losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable legal fees.

1.5 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means the statement of work attached as Annexure A and any subsequent statement of work executed by the Parties.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 The Service Provider shall provide the services described in the SOW ("**Services**"), namely the design and build of a collections analytics data mart, an early-warning risk scorecard, and Power BI dashboards for collections and portfolio monitoring.

2.2 The Company's representative is Mr. Mohan Krishnan.

2.3 The Service Provider shall comply with the Company's IT, information security and outsourcing policies as notified in writing.

## 3. COMPANY OBLIGATIONS

3.1 The Company shall provide access to masked or production Company Data, its loan management system, and business users within the timelines specified in the SOW.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The total fees for the Services are **USD 48,000** (United States Dollars Forty-Eight Thousand only), payable on completion of Milestones as set out in Annexure A.

4.2 The Service Provider shall raise an invoice upon completion of each Milestone. The Company shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt of the invoice.

4.3 All fees are exclusive of GST. The Company shall be entitled to deduct TDS as applicable.

4.4 Any disputed amount shall be notified within ten (10) days of receipt of invoice, and the Parties shall resolve such dispute in good faith within thirty (30) days.

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in force for one (1) year, unless terminated earlier or extended by mutual written agreement.

5.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice.

5.3 The Company may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' notice, subject to payment for Services rendered up to the date of termination, including a pro rata portion of the Milestone Fee for any Milestone in progress.

5.4 The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately if directed by the Reserve Bank of India or any other regulator.

## 6. ACCEPTANCE

6.1 The Company shall review each Deliverable within ten (10) business days and either accept it or provide written reasons for rejection. Deliverables shall be deemed accepted if no response is received within that period.

## 7. DELAY IN DELIVERY

7.1 If the Service Provider fails to complete any Milestone by the date specified in the SOW for reasons solely attributable to the Service Provider, the Service Provider shall pay liquidated damages of 0.5% (zero point five percent) of the Milestone Fee of the delayed Milestone for each completed week of delay.

7.2 The aggregate liquidated damages for any Milestone shall not exceed 5% (five percent) of the Milestone Fee of that Milestone.

7.3 No liquidated damages shall be payable for any delay attributable to the Company, including delay in provision of data, access, approvals, or feedback, or to a Force Majeure event, and the Milestone dates shall be extended accordingly.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each Party shall hold the other Party's confidential information in strict confidence and shall not disclose it except to its employees, auditors and advisers on a need-to-know basis.

8.2 The Service Provider acknowledges that Company Data includes customer information protected under the RBI Master Direction – Know Your Customer and shall not use it for any purpose other than performing the Services.

8.3 This Clause 8 shall survive for five (5) years after termination.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 Upon full payment, intellectual property in the Deliverables developed specifically for the Company shall vest in the Company.

9.2 The Service Provider retains its pre-existing intellectual property and grants the Company a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use it as incorporated in the Deliverables.

## 10. WARRANTIES

10.1 The Service Provider warrants that the Services shall be performed diligently and in a professional manner, and that the Deliverables shall materially conform to the SOW for sixty (60) days after acceptance.

## 11. INDEMNITY

11.1 Each Party shall indemnify the other against Losses arising from third-party claims to the extent caused by its breach of confidentiality or data protection obligations, infringement of third-party intellectual property rights, or gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, consequential or special damages, or loss of profit.

12.2 The aggregate liability of either Party under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement.

## 13. DATA PROTECTION AND AUDIT

13.1 The Service Provider shall comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable RBI directions in processing Company Data, and shall store Company Data only in India.

13.2 The Service Provider shall report any security incident to the Company within six (6) hours of detection.

13.3 The Company, its auditors and the Reserve Bank of India shall have the right to inspect and audit the Service Provider's records and facilities relating to the Services.

## 14. FORCE MAJEURE

14.1 Neither Party shall be liable for failure or delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.

15.2 Any dispute not resolved amicably within thirty (30) days shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Mumbai. The language of arbitration shall be English.

15.3 Subject to Clause 15.2, the courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 Neither Party shall assign or subcontract this Agreement without prior written consent of the other.

16.2 This Agreement with its Annexures constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

16.3 Amendments shall be in writing and signed by both Parties.
