# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**Date:** 22 January 2025

## PARTIES

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made between:

1. **AL NOOR HOSPITALITY GROUP LLC**, a limited liability company registered in the Emirate of Dubai, United Arab Emirates, having its principal office at Level 22, Al Noor Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road, PO Box 118834, Dubai, United Arab Emirates ("**Al Noor**"); and

2. **ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a private limited company incorporated under the laws of India (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456; GSTIN 27AAKCA4821F1Z3), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India ("**AtliQ**"),

each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## RECITALS

A. The Parties are discussing a possible engagement under which AtliQ would design and implement a guest analytics platform for Al Noor's hotel portfolio (the "**Purpose**").

B. In the course of those discussions each Party expects to disclose to the other certain confidential information, and each Party wishes to protect its information on equal terms.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means any information disclosed by one Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the date of this Agreement, in any form, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including guest and occupancy data, pricing, commercial proposals, solution architecture, source code, methodologies and business plans.

1.2 "**Representatives**" means a Party's directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and subcontractors who need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

2.1 Each Party, as Receiving Party, shall:
   (a) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
   (b) not disclose it to any third party other than its Representatives who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement;
   (c) protect it using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; and
   (d) promptly notify the Disclosing Party on becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure.

2.2 Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

## 3. EXCLUSIONS

3.1 The obligations in Clause 2 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:
   (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement;
   (b) was lawfully in its possession before disclosure by the Disclosing Party;
   (c) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or
   (d) is independently developed without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

3.2 A Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, regulation or order of a competent court or authority, provided that (where lawful) it gives the Disclosing Party prompt prior notice and discloses only the minimum required.

## 4. PERSONAL DATA

4.1 Where Confidential Information includes personal data of guests or employees, the Receiving Party shall process it only as necessary for the Purpose and in accordance with applicable data protection law, including the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 and the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as applicable.

## 5. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

5.1 On written request of the Disclosing Party, or on termination of discussions, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, save for copies retained in routine electronic back-ups or as required by law, which remain subject to this Agreement.

## 6. TERM

6.1 This Agreement applies to disclosures made during the period of two (2) years from its date. Either Party may end that period earlier by thirty (30) days' written notice.

6.2 The obligations in Clauses 2 to 5 shall continue for five (5) years from the date of each disclosure.

## 7. NO LICENCE; NO OBLIGATION

7.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right is granted except as expressly stated.

7.2 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.

## 8. REMEDIES

8.1 Each Party acknowledges that damages may not be an adequate remedy for breach, and that the Disclosing Party may seek injunctive or equitable relief in addition to any other remedy.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties.

9.2 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other.

9.3 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in Mumbai, conducted in English.

9.4 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
