# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("**Agreement**") is executed at Chennai on this 10th day of March 2025 ("**Effective Date**")

**BY AND BETWEEN**

**ACME RETAIL GROUP PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at No. 45, Anna Salai, Teynampet, Chennai 600018, Tamil Nadu, India (hereinafter referred to as the "**Client**", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include its successors and permitted assigns) of the ONE PART;

**AND**

**ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456; GSTIN 27AAKCA4821F1Z3), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India (hereinafter referred to as the "**Service Provider**", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include its successors and permitted assigns) of the OTHER PART.

## RECITALS

WHEREAS the Client operates a chain of retail stores in South India and wishes to implement enterprise Power BI dashboards for sales, inventory and store performance reporting;

WHEREAS the Service Provider is engaged in the business of software development, data analytics and business intelligence services; and

WHEREAS the Parties wish to record the terms on which the Service Provider will render such services.

NOW THEREFORE THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Deliverables**" means the dashboards, data models, reports, documentation and other work product specified in a Statement of Work.

1.2 "**Fees**" means the fees set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

1.3 "**Milestone**" and "**Milestone Fee**" mean a milestone and the fee attributable to it, as specified in a Statement of Work.

1.4 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a document executed by both Parties describing specific services, Deliverables, Milestones and Fees.

1.5 "**Confidential Information**" means all business, technical, financial and customer information disclosed by either Party, whether oral, written or electronic, that is designated as confidential or ought reasonably to be understood as confidential.

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 The Service Provider shall provide the services described in each SOW ("**Services**"). SOW-1 dated 10 March 2025 covers the design and implementation of Power BI dashboards for sales, inventory and store performance across the Client's retail network.

2.2 Any change in scope shall be effected only through a written change request signed by both Parties.

2.3 The Client's single point of contact shall be Ms. Priya Raman.

## 3. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS

3.1 The Client shall provide timely access to its ERP and point-of-sale data, Power BI tenant, and relevant personnel, and shall review and respond to Deliverables within the timelines in the SOW.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The total Fees under SOW-1 are **USD 38,000** (United States Dollars Thirty-Eight Thousand only), payable against Milestones as specified in SOW-1.

4.2 The Service Provider shall raise invoices upon completion of each Milestone. The Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

4.3 Fees are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax ("**GST**"), which shall be charged at the applicable rate. The Client may deduct tax at source as required under the Income Tax Act, 1961 and shall furnish TDS certificates within the statutory timelines.

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and remain in force for a period of two (2) years, unless terminated earlier.

5.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure it within such period.

5.3 The Client may terminate an SOW for convenience upon thirty (30) days' written notice, subject to payment for Services rendered and Milestones completed or in progress (pro rata) up to the date of termination.

## 6. ACCEPTANCE

6.1 The Client shall test each Deliverable within seven (7) business days of delivery. A Deliverable shall be deemed accepted if no written notice of material non-conformity is received within that period.

## 7. DELAY AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

7.1 If the Service Provider fails to complete a Milestone by the agreed date for reasons solely attributable to the Service Provider, the Service Provider shall pay liquidated damages at the rate of 0.5% (half percent) of the Milestone Fee of the delayed Milestone for each complete week of delay.

7.2 The aggregate liquidated damages in respect of any Milestone shall not exceed 5% (five percent) of the Milestone Fee of that Milestone.

7.3 No liquidated damages shall be payable where the delay is caused by the Client, including delayed access to data or systems, delayed review of Deliverables, or changes in scope, or by a Force Majeure event.

7.4 The Parties agree that the liquidated damages are a genuine pre-estimate of loss and shall be the Client's sole and exclusive remedy for delay.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each Party shall maintain the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party except to its employees and professional advisers on a need-to-know basis.

8.2 These obligations shall survive for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 Upon receipt of full payment, all intellectual property rights in the Deliverables created specifically for the Client shall vest in the Client.

9.2 The Service Provider shall retain all rights in its pre-existing intellectual property, tools, templates and know-how, and grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use any such pre-existing materials incorporated into the Deliverables for the Client's internal business purposes.

## 10. WARRANTIES

10.1 The Service Provider warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional manner by qualified personnel, and that the Deliverables shall conform materially to the SOW for sixty (60) days from acceptance.

## 11. INDEMNITY

11.1 Each Party ("**Indemnifying Party**") shall indemnify the other against losses arising from third-party claims caused by the Indemnifying Party's breach of confidentiality, infringement of third-party intellectual property rights, or gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, including loss of profits or business.

12.2 The total aggregate liability of either Party under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Fees paid or payable under the SOW giving rise to the claim.

## 13. DATA PROTECTION

13.1 The Service Provider shall process personal data received from the Client only for the purpose of the Services and in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made thereunder, as and when in force, and shall implement reasonable security practices.

## 14. NON-SOLICITATION

14.1 During the term and for six (6) months thereafter, neither Party shall solicit any employee of the other Party who was involved in the Services.

## 15. FORCE MAJEURE

15.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, epidemics, war, strikes or governmental action.

## 16. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

16.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.

16.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably within thirty (30) days. Failing such resolution, the dispute shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator mutually appointed by the Parties, in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Mumbai. The language of arbitration shall be English.

16.3 Subject to Clause 16.2, the courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 17. MISCELLANEOUS

17.1 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other.

17.2 This Agreement, together with all SOWs, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

17.3 Any amendment shall be valid only if made in writing and signed by both Parties.

17.4 Notices shall be addressed to the registered offices stated above, with a copy by email to the Parties' designated contacts.
