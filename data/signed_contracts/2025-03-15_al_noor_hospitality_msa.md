# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement No.:** ANHG/IT/2025/017
**Date:** 15 March 2025

## PARTIES

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on the date stated above between:

1. **AL NOOR HOSPITALITY GROUP LLC**, a limited liability company registered in the Emirate of Dubai, United Arab Emirates, having its principal office at Level 22, Al Noor Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road, PO Box 118834, Dubai, United Arab Emirates (the "**Client**"); and

2. **ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a private limited company incorporated under the laws of India (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456; GSTIN 27AAKCA4821F1Z3), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India (the "**Supplier**"),

each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## RECITALS

A. The Client owns, operates and manages a portfolio of hotels, resorts and serviced apartments in the United Arab Emirates and wishes to implement a centralised guest analytics platform across fourteen (14) of its properties.

B. The Supplier is engaged in the business of providing software development, data engineering, analytics and artificial intelligence services and has represented that it has the skills and resources to deliver such platform.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which the Supplier will provide the Services to the Client, including certain restrictions reasonably necessary to protect the Client's commercially sensitive information and competitive position.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, capitalised terms have the meanings given below.

1.2 "**Affiliate**" means, in relation to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party, where "control" means the ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies, whether by contract or otherwise.

1.3 "**Business Day**" means a day other than a Friday, Saturday or public holiday in Dubai.

1.4 "**Client Data**" means all data, including Guest Data, reservation, occupancy, pricing and revenue data, supplied by or on behalf of the Client or generated by the Platform in the course of the Services.

1.5 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Clause 8.1.

1.6 "**Deliverables**" means all documents, software, dashboards, models, reports and other materials delivered by the Supplier to the Client under an SOW.

1.7 "**Effective Date**" means 15 March 2025.

1.8 "**Fees**" means the fees payable by the Client for the Services as set out in Clause 5 and the applicable SOW.

1.9 "**GCC**" means the member states of the Gulf Cooperation Council, being the United Arab Emirates, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, the State of Qatar, the State of Kuwait, the Kingdom of Bahrain and the Sultanate of Oman.

1.10 "**Guest Data**" means any personal data relating to guests, prospective guests or loyalty members of the Client.

1.11 "**Hospitality Business**" means any person, firm or entity that owns, operates, manages or franchises hotels, resorts or serviced apartments, or that provides distribution, revenue-management or guest-analytics services to any such person, firm or entity.

1.12 "**Milestone**" means a delivery milestone specified in an SOW.

1.13 "**Platform**" means the guest analytics platform to be designed, built and deployed by the Supplier for the Client as described in SOW-1.

1.14 "**Restricted Period**" means the Term and the period of eighteen (18) months following the expiry or termination of this Agreement.

1.15 "**Services**" means the services described in this Agreement and each SOW.

1.16 "**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by both Parties referencing this Agreement.

1.17 Clause headings are for convenience only. References to "including" mean "including without limitation". The singular includes the plural and vice versa.

## 2. APPOINTMENT AND SCOPE

2.1 The Client appoints the Supplier to provide the Services, and the Supplier agrees to provide the Services, on the terms of this Agreement and each SOW.

2.2 The initial SOW ("**SOW-1**") is attached as Schedule 1 and covers the design, build, deployment and hypercare of the Platform across the fourteen (14) properties listed in Schedule 1, including integration with the Client's property management system, central reservation system and loyalty database, guest segmentation models, and executive dashboards.

2.3 In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and an SOW, this Agreement shall prevail unless the SOW expressly states that it overrides a specified clause of this Agreement.

## 3. CHANGE CONTROL

3.1 Either Party may request a change to an SOW by written change request. No change shall be binding unless agreed in a written change order signed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

3.2 The Supplier shall, within ten (10) Business Days of a change request, provide an impact assessment covering effort, timeline and Fees.

## 4. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS

4.1 The Client shall provide the Supplier with timely access to its systems, data, premises and personnel as reasonably required, and shall nominate a single point of contact, who shall initially be Mr. Omar Haddad, Head of Digital.

4.2 The Client shall be responsible for obtaining any third-party consents required for the Supplier to access the Client's systems.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 The total Fees for SOW-1 are **USD 185,000** (one hundred and eighty-five thousand United States Dollars), payable against Milestones as set out in Schedule 1.

5.2 The Supplier shall invoice upon achievement of each Milestone. Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt.

5.3 All Fees are exclusive of VAT and any other applicable taxes. Where the Client is required by law to withhold tax, it shall provide the Supplier with the relevant withholding certificate.

5.4 Late payments shall bear interest at one percent (1%) per month from the due date until the date of payment.

5.5 The Client may dispute any invoice in good faith by written notice within ten (10) Business Days of receipt, and shall pay the undisputed portion by the due date.

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for a period of three (3) years, expiring on 14 March 2028 (the "**Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Clause 6.

6.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice with immediate effect if the other Party commits a material breach which, if remediable, is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice.

6.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other Party becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation or has a receiver appointed.

6.4 The Client may terminate an SOW for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice, subject to payment for all Services performed up to the date of termination and any non-cancellable costs.

6.5 Clauses 8, 9, 12, 14, 15, 16 and 21 shall survive expiry or termination of this Agreement.

## 7. PERSONNEL

7.1 The Supplier shall assign suitably qualified personnel and shall not replace key personnel named in an SOW without the Client's prior consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

7.2 Supplier personnel attending the Client's premises shall comply with the Client's site, health and safety and information security policies.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all information disclosed by one Party to the other, whether before or after the Effective Date, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be regarded as confidential, including Client Data, pricing strategies, rate plans, occupancy forecasts and business plans.

8.2 Each Party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to its employees and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

8.3 These obligations do not apply to information that is public other than through breach, was lawfully known before disclosure, is independently developed, or is required to be disclosed by law.

8.4 The obligations in this Clause 8 continue for five (5) years after expiry or termination.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 Upon payment in full of the relevant Fees, all intellectual property rights in the Deliverables created specifically for the Client shall vest in the Client.

9.2 The Supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, libraries, methodologies and know-how ("**Supplier Background IP**"). To the extent any Supplier Background IP is incorporated into a Deliverable, the Supplier grants the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use it as part of that Deliverable.

## 10. DELIVERY, ACCEPTANCE AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

10.1 The Supplier shall use all reasonable endeavours to achieve each Milestone by the date set out in the relevant SOW.

10.2 Each Deliverable shall be subject to acceptance testing by the Client within ten (10) Business Days of delivery against the acceptance criteria in the SOW. Deliverables not rejected in writing within that period shall be deemed accepted.

10.3 If the Supplier fails to achieve a Milestone by its due date for reasons attributable to the Supplier, the Supplier shall pay to the Client, as liquidated damages, one percent (1%) of the Fees payable for the affected Milestone for each complete week of delay, provided that the aggregate liquidated damages payable under this Agreement shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the total Fees payable under this Agreement.

10.4 The Parties agree that the liquidated damages in Clause 10.3 are a genuine pre-estimate of loss. No liquidated damages shall be payable for delay caused by the Client, its contractors, or a Force Majeure Event.

## 11. WARRANTIES

11.1 The Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care in accordance with good industry practice, and that the Deliverables will materially conform to their specifications for ninety (90) days after acceptance.

11.2 Each Party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement.

11.3 Except as expressly set out in this Agreement, all other warranties are excluded to the extent permitted by law.

## 12. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

12.1 In consideration of the Client disclosing to the Supplier its Confidential Information, including its revenue strategies, pricing models and Guest Data, the Supplier undertakes that neither it nor any of its Affiliates shall, during the Restricted Period, directly or indirectly, provide data analytics, artificial intelligence or revenue-management services to any Hospitality Business in the GCC.

12.2 For the purposes of Clause 12.1, services are provided "in the GCC" if the Hospitality Business receiving them is headquartered in, or the services relate to properties or operations located in, any state of the GCC, irrespective of where the services are performed.

12.3 The Supplier shall procure that each of its Affiliates complies with this Clause 12 as if it were a party to this Agreement, and any breach by an Affiliate shall be deemed a breach by the Supplier.

12.4 The Client may, in its sole discretion, waive the restriction in Clause 12.1 in respect of a specific engagement by written consent signed by an authorised signatory of the Client. Any such waiver shall be limited to its express terms.

12.5 During the Term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither Party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other Party who was directly involved in the Services, save through general advertisements not targeted at such employees.

12.6 The Supplier acknowledges that the restrictions in this Clause 12 are reasonable in scope, duration and geography and are necessary to protect the legitimate interests of the Client.

## 13. SUBCONTRACTING

13.1 The Supplier shall not subcontract any part of the Services without the Client's prior written consent. The Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## 14. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

14.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, consequential or special loss, or for loss of profits, revenue, goodwill or anticipated savings.

14.2 Subject to Clause 14.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to the Fees paid by the Client to the Supplier in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

14.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, breach of Clause 8 (Confidentiality), or death or personal injury caused by negligence.

## 15. INDEMNITY

15.1 The Supplier shall indemnify the Client against losses arising from any third-party claim that the Deliverables (excluding Client materials) infringe that third party's intellectual property rights.

15.2 The Client shall indemnify the Supplier against losses arising from any third-party claim that Client Data or Client materials infringe that third party's rights.

15.3 The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party, allow it conduct of the defence, and provide reasonable assistance.

## 16. DATA PROTECTION

16.1 Each Party shall comply with applicable data protection laws, including the DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020 and the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, to the extent applicable.

16.2 The Supplier shall process Guest Data only on the documented instructions of the Client, implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, and notify the Client without undue delay (and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours) of any personal data breach.

16.3 The Supplier shall not transfer Guest Data outside the UAE without the Client's prior written consent. Any Guest Data accessed from India shall be accessed remotely from UAE-hosted environments only.

## 17. INSURANCE

17.1 The Supplier shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of not less than USD 500,000 per claim for the Term and one (1) year thereafter.

## 18. FORCE MAJEURE

18.1 Neither Party shall be liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control (a "**Force Majeure Event**"), provided it notifies the other Party promptly. If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than sixty (60) days, either Party may terminate the affected SOW.

## 19. ASSIGNMENT

19.1 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except that the Client may assign to an Affiliate on written notice.

## 20. NOTICES

20.1 Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier or email to: (a) Client: Attn. Head of Digital, Level 22, Al Noor Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai, with copy to legal@alnoorhospitality.ae; (b) Supplier: Attn. Chief Executive Officer, 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, India, with copy to karandeep@atliq.com.

## 21. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

21.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of it shall be governed by the laws of the Dubai International Financial Centre.

21.2 The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation between senior executives for thirty (30) days.

21.3 Failing resolution, the courts of the Dubai International Financial Centre shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 22. GENERAL

22.1 This Agreement, together with its Schedules and SOWs, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

22.2 No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right shall operate as a waiver.

22.3 If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall continue in effect, and the invalid provision shall be deemed modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

22.4 The Parties are independent contractors. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
