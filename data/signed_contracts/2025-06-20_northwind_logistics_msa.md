6.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years unless terminated earlier in accordance with this clause 6.

## SCHEDULE 1 — STATEMENT OF WORK 1 (SOW-1)

**Title:** Operations Data Pipeline Modernisation
**SOW Date:** 20 June 2025
**Client Owner:** Tom Beckett, Head of Operations
**Supplier Owner:** Nikhil, Delivery Lead

**S1.1 Objectives.** Replace the Client's legacy nightly batch scripts with an automated, monitored data pipeline ingesting warehouse management system (WMS), telematics and transport management system (TMS) data into the Client's Azure data platform, and deliver daily operational KPI reporting.

**S1.2 Approach.** The Supplier will build the pipeline using its proprietary pipeline accelerator, **PipeKit**, which provides pre-built ingestion connectors, orchestration templates, data-quality checks and monitoring components. PipeKit will be configured and extended for the Client and incorporated into the delivered pipeline as its ingestion and orchestration layer.

**S1.3 Deliverables.**
(a) Source-system data audit and target data model;
(b) Production pipeline (incorporating PipeKit) for WMS, telematics and TMS sources;
(c) Data-quality rules and alerting;
(d) Operational KPI dashboards (on-time dispatch, dock utilisation, fleet idle time);
(e) Runbook, technical documentation and handover training.

**S1.4 Milestones and Charges.**

| Milestone | Description | Milestone Date | Milestone Fee (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Data audit and target model | 25 Jul 2025 | 10,800 |
| M2 | PipeKit-based pipeline build (WMS + TMS) | 26 Sep 2025 | 21,600 |
| M3 | Telematics integration, DQ rules, KPI dashboards | 28 Nov 2025 | 16,200 |
| M4 | Handover, documentation, training | 19 Dec 2025 | 5,400 |
| | **Total** | | **54,000** |

**S1.5 Client Dependencies.** Read access to WMS and TMS databases by 7 July 2025; telematics API credentials by 1 September 2025; availability of a Client data owner for weekly reviews.

**S1.6 Acceptance.** Each Milestone is accepted on written sign-off by the Client Owner or ten (10) Business Days after delivery without written rejection.
