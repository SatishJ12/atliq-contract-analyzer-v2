## Notes from finance
- Delivery teams for both entities sit in Pune; AtliQ Inc sub-contracts delivery to AtliQ Technologies Pvt Ltd under an intercompany agreement.
- The entity named in a client contract determines which entity invoices, which tax regime applies (GST vs US), and which bank account receives payment. Mismatches between the contracting entity and the invoicing entity have caused payment delays twice in 2025.
