# EXHIBIT D — BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

**Harrington Health Systems, Inc. — Contract No. HHS-IT-2026-0193, Exhibit D**
**Form: HHS Business Associate Agreement (Rev. 03/2026)**

This Business Associate Agreement (this "**BAA**") is entered into as of the Effective Date of the Master Services Agreement (as defined below) by and between **Harrington Health Systems, Inc.**, a Texas nonprofit corporation with its principal place of business at 6400 Fannin Street, Suite 1800, Houston, Texas 77030, on behalf of itself and its Affiliates that are covered entities ("**Covered Entity**" or "**Harrington**"), and **AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Business Associate**").

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Covered Entity and Business Associate are parties to the Master Professional Services Agreement, Contract No. HHS-IT-2026-0193 (together with all Statements of Work and Exhibits thereto, the "**Master Services Agreement**"), under which Business Associate will perform services that involve the creation, receipt, maintenance or transmission of Protected Health Information on behalf of Covered Entity;

WHEREAS, Covered Entity is required by HIPAA to obtain satisfactory assurances that Business Associate will appropriately safeguard Protected Health Information;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises herein and in the Master Services Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**HIPAA**" means the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act (Title XIII of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009), and the regulations at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160, 162 and 164, each as amended.
1.2 "**Protected Health Information**" or "**PHI**" has the meaning given at 45 C.F.R. § 160.103, limited to information created, received, maintained or transmitted by Business Associate or any Subcontractor on behalf of Covered Entity, and includes electronic PHI.
1.3 "**Security Incident**" has the meaning given at 45 C.F.R. § 164.304.
1.4 "**Breach**" has the meaning given at 45 C.F.R. § 164.402.
1.5 "**Subcontractor**" means any person or entity, including any Affiliate of Business Associate, to whom Business Associate delegates a function, activity or service under the Master Services Agreement, other than in the capacity of a member of Business Associate's workforce.
1.6 "**Privacy Event**" means any (a) Security Incident; (b) Breach; (c) use or disclosure of PHI not permitted by this BAA; or (d) suspected occurrence of any of the foregoing.
1.7 "**Texas Privacy Law**" means the Texas Medical Records Privacy Act (Tex. Health & Safety Code ch. 181) and Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 521.
1.8 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this BAA have the meanings given in HIPAA or, if not defined there, in the Master Services Agreement.

## ARTICLE 2 — PERMITTED USES AND DISCLOSURES

2.1 Business Associate may use or disclose PHI solely as necessary to perform the Services under the Master Services Agreement, in compliance with this BAA and the minimum necessary standard at 45 C.F.R. § 164.502(b).
2.2 Business Associate may use PHI for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities, and may disclose PHI for such purposes only if Required By Law or if Business Associate obtains reasonable written assurances from the recipient as required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(4).
2.3 Business Associate shall not (a) sell PHI; (b) use or disclose PHI for marketing or fundraising; (c) perform Data Aggregation services except as expressly authorized in writing by Covered Entity; or (d) use or disclose PHI in any manner that would violate Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 if done by Covered Entity.
2.4 Business Associate shall not de-identify PHI, or use de-identified information derived from PHI for any purpose other than the Services, without Covered Entity's prior written approval.

## ARTICLE 3 — SAFEGUARDS

3.1 Business Associate shall implement administrative, physical and technical safeguards that reasonably and appropriately protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of electronic PHI, and shall comply with Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164.
3.2 Business Associate shall comply with the Client Information Security Requirements set forth in Exhibit B to the Master Services Agreement, including encryption of PHI at rest and in transit, multi-factor authentication, and logging and monitoring of all access to PHI.
3.3 Business Associate shall ensure that all members of its workforce with access to PHI have completed HIPAA privacy and security training prior to access and annually thereafter, and shall provide training records upon request.
3.4 Business Associate shall apply sanctions against members of its workforce who fail to comply with its privacy and security policies, consistent with 45 C.F.R. § 164.530(e), and shall promptly revoke access to PHI upon termination or reassignment of any such person.
3.5 Business Associate shall maintain a documented security risk analysis covering all systems used to access PHI in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A), and shall update it at least annually.

## ARTICLE 4 — LOCATION OF PHI; SUBCONTRACTORS

4.1 **Domestic Performance.** Business Associate shall not, and shall ensure that no Subcontractor shall, access, view, create, receive, maintain, transmit, store or process PHI from or at any location outside the United States, whether directly or by remote access.
4.2 **Subcontractor Requirements.** Business Associate shall not engage any Subcontractor in connection with the Services without Covered Entity's prior written approval of such Subcontractor. Each approved Subcontractor shall be organized under the laws of a State of the United States, and all of its personnel performing Services shall be located in the United States.
4.3 **Flow-Down.** In accordance with 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), Business Associate shall ensure that each Subcontractor agrees in writing, prior to any access to PHI, to the same restrictions, conditions and requirements that apply to Business Associate under this BAA. Business Associate shall provide a copy of each such agreement to Covered Entity upon request.
4.4 Business Associate shall remain fully responsible for the acts and omissions of its Subcontractors as if they were Business Associate's own.

## ARTICLE 5 — REPORTING OF PRIVACY EVENTS

5.1 **Notice.** Business Associate shall notify Covered Entity's Privacy Officer by telephone and in writing of any Privacy Event within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery. A Privacy Event shall be deemed discovered on the first day on which it is known, or by exercising reasonable diligence would have been known, to any employee, officer, agent or Subcontractor of Business Associate.
5.2 **Content.** Business Associate's notice shall include, to the extent known, the information described at 45 C.F.R. § 164.410(c), and Business Associate shall supplement such notice within five (5) days and thereafter as information becomes available.
5.3 **Unsuccessful Attempts.** Notice is hereby deemed given for unsuccessful Security Incidents, such as pings, port scans and denied log-in attempts, that do not result in unauthorized access, use or disclosure of PHI.
5.4 **Cooperation.** Business Associate shall cooperate fully in Covered Entity's investigation of any Privacy Event and shall not notify any Individual, regulator or media outlet of a Privacy Event without Covered Entity's prior written consent, except as Required By Law.
5.5 **Mitigation.** Business Associate shall promptly mitigate, at its own expense, any harmful effect of a Privacy Event.

## ARTICLE 6 — INDIVIDUAL RIGHTS

6.1 Within five (5) business days of Covered Entity's request, Business Associate shall make PHI maintained in a Designated Record Set available to Covered Entity as necessary to satisfy 45 C.F.R. § 164.524, including in electronic form where requested.
6.2 Within five (5) business days of Covered Entity's request, Business Associate shall incorporate any amendment to PHI in a Designated Record Set as directed by Covered Entity under 45 C.F.R. § 164.526.
6.3 Business Associate shall document disclosures of PHI and provide to Covered Entity, within ten (10) business days of request, the information required for an accounting of disclosures under 45 C.F.R. § 164.528.
6.4 Business Associate shall forward to Covered Entity within three (3) business days any request received directly from an Individual.

## ARTICLE 7 — COMPLIANCE AND AUDIT

7.1 **HHS Access.** Business Associate shall make its internal practices, books and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary for purposes of determining Covered Entity's compliance with HIPAA.
7.2 **Covered Entity Audit.** Covered Entity or its designee may inspect and audit Business Associate's and any Subcontractor's facilities, systems, books, records, policies and procedures relating to PHI upon forty-eight (48) hours' notice, at any time during the term of this BAA and for so long as Business Associate retains any PHI. Business Associate shall promptly remediate any deficiency identified.
7.3 **Texas Privacy Law.** Business Associate shall comply with Texas Privacy Law to the extent applicable to Business Associate, including training requirements for employees who handle PHI.
7.4 **Litigation Support.** Business Associate shall make itself and its workforce reasonably available to Covered Entity, at no cost, to testify in any litigation or administrative proceeding against Covered Entity relating to Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI, except where Business Associate is a named adverse party.

## ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION; INSURANCE

8.1 **Indemnification.** Business Associate shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Covered Entity, its Affiliates and their respective trustees, officers, employees and agents from and against any and all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any Privacy Event involving Business Associate or any Subcontractor; (b) any breach of this BAA by Business Associate or any Subcontractor; or (c) any violation of HIPAA or Texas Privacy Law by Business Associate or any Subcontractor, including without limitation regulatory fines, civil monetary penalties, costs of notification of Individuals, regulators and media, call-center support, credit monitoring and identity theft protection for affected Individuals, forensic investigation, and reasonable attorneys' fees.
8.2 **No Limitation.** The obligations of Business Associate under this Article 8 are not subject to any limitation or exclusion of liability, and shall apply notwithstanding any limitation of liability in the Master Services Agreement.
8.3 **Insurance.** Business Associate shall maintain, throughout the term of this BAA and for three (3) years thereafter, cyber liability and network security and privacy insurance with limits of not less than Five Million U.S. Dollars ($5,000,000) per claim and in the aggregate, covering notification costs, regulatory defense and penalties, and third-party claims arising from Privacy Events, and shall name Covered Entity as an additional insured.

## ARTICLE 9 — OBLIGATIONS OF COVERED ENTITY

9.1 Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of (a) any limitation in its notice of privacy practices under 45 C.F.R. § 164.520; (b) any change in, or revocation of, an Individual's authorization to use or disclose PHI; and (c) any restriction on the use or disclosure of PHI agreed to by Covered Entity under 45 C.F.R. § 164.522, in each case to the extent it may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.
9.2 Covered Entity shall not request Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any manner that would not be permissible under HIPAA if done by Covered Entity, except as permitted by Section 2.2.
9.3 Covered Entity shall provide PHI to Business Associate only through the secure transfer methods or Client-controlled environments specified in Exhibit B to the Master Services Agreement.

## ARTICLE 10 — OWNERSHIP OF PHI; ELECTRONIC TRANSACTIONS

10.1 As between the Parties, all PHI and all data derived from PHI is and shall remain the sole property of Covered Entity. Business Associate acquires no right, title or interest in PHI by reason of this BAA or the Master Services Agreement.
10.2 To the extent Business Associate conducts any Standard Transaction on behalf of Covered Entity, Business Associate shall comply with 45 C.F.R. Part 162 and shall require any Subcontractor to do the same.
10.3 Business Associate shall not use PHI, or any data derived from PHI, to train, tune or evaluate any machine learning or artificial intelligence model other than models developed exclusively for Covered Entity under the Master Services Agreement.
10.4 Business Associate shall maintain policies and procedures, and documentation of all actions and assessments required by Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164, for six (6) years from the later of creation or last effective date, and shall make them available to Covered Entity on request.

### Limited Data Sets

10.5 If Covered Entity provides Business Associate with a Limited Data Set, as defined at 45 C.F.R. § 164.514(e)(2), Business Associate shall (a) use or disclose the Limited Data Set only for the Services; (b) not identify or attempt to identify the information or contact any Individual; (c) use appropriate safeguards to prevent other use or disclosure; and (d) ensure that any Subcontractor to whom it provides the Limited Data Set agrees to the same restrictions. This Section constitutes a data use agreement for purposes of 45 C.F.R. § 164.514(e)(4).

## ARTICLE 11 — TERM AND TERMINATION

11.1 **Term.** This BAA shall become effective on the Effective Date of the Master Services Agreement and shall remain in effect until all PHI provided by Covered Entity to Business Associate, or created, received or maintained by Business Associate or any Subcontractor on behalf of Covered Entity, has been returned to Covered Entity or destroyed and such return or destruction has been certified in writing by Business Associate, regardless of the expiration or termination of the Master Services Agreement.
11.2 **Termination for Breach.** If Covered Entity determines that Business Associate has violated a material term of this BAA, Covered Entity may, at its option, (a) provide Business Associate an opportunity to cure within a period specified by Covered Entity, and terminate this BAA and the Master Services Agreement if Business Associate does not cure; or (b) immediately terminate this BAA and the Master Services Agreement if cure is not possible.
11.3 **Return or Destruction.** Upon termination or expiration of the Master Services Agreement, Business Associate shall, within thirty (30) days, return or destroy all PHI, including PHI in the possession of Subcontractors, retain no copies, and certify destruction in writing. If return or destruction is infeasible, Business Associate shall extend the protections of this BAA to such PHI and limit further uses and disclosures to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible.
11.4 **Survival.** Articles 5, 7, 8, 10 and 11 shall survive any expiration or termination of this BAA or the Master Services Agreement.

## ARTICLE 12 — MISCELLANEOUS

12.1 **Amendment.** The Parties shall take such action as is necessary to amend this BAA to comply with any change in HIPAA. Covered Entity may amend this BAA by written notice to the extent required to comply with HIPAA.
12.2 **Interpretation.** Any ambiguity in this BAA shall be resolved to permit Covered Entity to comply with HIPAA. In the event of any conflict between this BAA and the Master Services Agreement with respect to PHI, this BAA shall control.
12.3 **No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this BAA confers any rights upon any person other than the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.
12.4 **Injunctive Relief.** Business Associate acknowledges that any breach of this BAA may cause Covered Entity irreparable harm, and that Covered Entity shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief without posting bond.
12.5 **Governing Law.** This BAA shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas and applicable federal law, and venue shall lie as provided in the Master Services Agreement.
12.6 **Notices.** Notices under this BAA shall be given as provided in the Master Services Agreement, with a copy of any notice under Article 5 to: Privacy Officer, Harrington Health Systems, Inc., 6400 Fannin Street, Suite 1800, Houston, Texas 77030 (privacy@harringtonhealth.org; Privacy Hotline (713) 555-0148).
12.7 **Relationship.** Business Associate is an independent contractor and is not an agent of Covered Entity for purposes of 45 C.F.R. § 160.402(c) or otherwise.
12.8 **Regulatory References.** A reference in this BAA to a section of HIPAA means the section as in effect or as amended, and for which compliance is required.
12.9 **Severability.** If any provision of this BAA is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
12.10 **Counterparts.** This BAA may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, and shall be executed concurrently with the Master Services Agreement.
