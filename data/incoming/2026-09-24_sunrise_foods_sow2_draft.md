## 3. Background

Under SOW No. 1, AtliQ delivered version 1 of the Sunrise distributor portal, which is used by approximately 140 distributors across Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka for order placement and scheme tracking. The Client now wishes to extend the portal and give its sales leadership a consolidated view of secondary sales performance.

## 4. Scope of Services

4.1 **Distributor Portal v2**
   (a) Credit-limit visibility and online payment reconciliation for distributors;
   (b) Scheme and promotion eligibility calculator;
   (c) Mobile-responsive redesign and Malayalam / Tamil language support;
   (d) Integration with the Client's ERP for real-time stock availability.

4.2 **Sales Performance Dashboard**
   (a) Power BI dashboard covering primary and secondary sales by region, distributor, SKU and salesperson;
   (b) Target-vs-actual tracking and scheme ROI analysis;
   (c) Weekly automated email summaries to regional sales managers.

4.3 **Out of scope:** retailer-level (tertiary) sales tracking, changes to the Client's ERP, and hosting costs (borne directly by the Client).
