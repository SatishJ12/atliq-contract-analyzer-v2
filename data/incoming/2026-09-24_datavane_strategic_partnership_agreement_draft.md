# STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

**Datavane, Inc. — Global Partner Program**
**Agreement No. DV-GPP-2026-0417 | Form: Datavane SPA (Implementation & Referral Partner) v4.2**

This Strategic Partnership Agreement (together with its Schedules, this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the date of the last signature below (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Datavane, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 575 Market Street, Suite 2200, San Francisco, California 94105, USA ("**Datavane**"); and

**AtliQ Technologies Pvt Ltd**, a private limited company incorporated under the laws of India, CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456, having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India ("**Partner**").

Datavane and Partner are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Datavane develops and offers the Datavane Lakehouse Platform, a cloud-native lakehouse and unified analytics platform offered on a subscription basis.

B. Partner provides data engineering, analytics, AI/ML and business intelligence services to enterprise customers.

C. Datavane wishes to appoint Partner as a non-exclusive implementation and referral partner for the Datavane Platform in the Territory, and Partner wishes to accept such appointment, on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity.
1.2 "**Datavane Platform**" or "**Platform**" means Datavane's lakehouse, data warehouse, streaming, governance and analytics software offered as a service, including all updates, modules and add-ons made generally available by Datavane.
1.3 "**Customer**" means any person or entity that purchases or evaluates a subscription to the Platform.
1.4 "**Prospect**" means any person or entity that Partner identifies, introduces, registers or refers to Datavane, or with which Partner engages in any discussion regarding the Platform, whether or not such person or entity was an existing client of Partner prior to the Effective Date.
1.5 "**Datavane Account**" means (a) each Customer, (b) each Prospect registered by Partner through the Partner Portal, and (c) each Prospect introduced to Datavane by Partner in any manner, together with such entity's Affiliates.
1.6 "**Joint Deal**" means any transaction under which a Datavane Account purchases a Platform subscription together with implementation, migration, managed or consulting services delivered in whole or in part by Partner.
1.7 "**Joint Deal Revenue**" means all amounts invoiced to a Datavane Account in respect of a Joint Deal, including Platform subscription fees and Partner Services fees, net of taxes.
1.8 "**Partner Services**" means implementation, migration, integration, managed and consulting services relating to the Platform performed by Partner for Datavane Accounts.
1.9 "**Partner Portal**" means Datavane's online partner relationship management system.
1.10 "**Territory**" means the Republic of India; the member states of the Gulf Cooperation Council (United Arab Emirates, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, State of Qatar, State of Kuwait, Kingdom of Bahrain and Sultanate of Oman); and Europe (the member states of the European Union and the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland).
1.11 "**Marks**" means a Party's trademarks, trade names, service marks and logos.
1.12 "**Losses**" means any and all losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, claims, actions, judgments, settlements, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs or expenses of whatever kind, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Datavane appoints Partner, and Partner accepts appointment, as a non-exclusive "Datavane Premier Implementation Partner" for the Territory.
2.2 **Scope of Activities.** Partner shall (a) actively promote the Platform to Prospects in the Territory; (b) register all Prospects in the Partner Portal within five (5) business days of first substantive contact; (c) deliver Partner Services to Datavane Accounts in accordance with Datavane's published implementation methodology; and (d) participate in joint marketing activities as agreed in the annual joint business plan.
2.3 **Non-Exclusivity.** Datavane may appoint other partners, resellers and service providers in the Territory and may sell directly to any Customer or Prospect, including Prospects registered by Partner.
2.4 **Affiliates.** Partner may perform Partner Services through its Affiliates, provided that Partner shall remain responsible for each Affiliate's compliance with this Agreement, and all obligations of Partner hereunder shall bind Partner's Affiliates.
2.5 **Deal Registration.** A Prospect registration is valid for ninety (90) days. Datavane may accept or reject any registration in its sole discretion. Acceptance of a registration does not guarantee that Partner will be engaged for any Joint Deal.

## 3. PARTNER OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Certified Personnel.** Partner shall maintain at least six (6) Datavane Certified Engineers and one (1) Datavane Certified Architect throughout the Term, and shall ensure that all Partner Services are led by a Datavane Certified Architect.
3.2 **Pipeline Commitment.** Partner shall use commercially reasonable efforts to register not fewer than twelve (12) qualified Prospects in each contract year.
3.3 **Conduct.** Partner shall not make any representation, warranty or commitment regarding the Platform other than as set out in Datavane's then-current published documentation.
3.4 **Compliance.** Partner shall comply with Datavane's Partner Code of Conduct, applicable anti-corruption laws (including the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and the UK Bribery Act 2010) and applicable export control and sanctions laws.
3.5 **Reporting.** Partner shall provide Datavane with a monthly pipeline report and a quarterly business review in the format reasonably specified by Datavane.

## 4. DATAVANE OBLIGATIONS

4.1 Datavane shall provide Partner with (a) access to the Partner Portal; (b) up to ten (10) free certification vouchers per contract year; (c) a non-production Platform sandbox environment; and (d) a named partner manager.
4.2 Datavane may, in its discretion, provide Partner with pre-sales support, marketing development funds and co-marketing materials.

## 5. DATAVANE ACCOUNTS

5.1 **Ownership of Relationship.** Partner acknowledges that each Datavane Account is a customer relationship of Datavane. All information relating to Datavane Accounts, including contact details, requirements, usage data and pricing, shall be Datavane's Confidential Information.
5.2 **Registration of Existing Clients.** Where Partner proposes the Platform to an existing client of Partner, Partner shall register such client in the Partner Portal as a Prospect, whereupon such client shall become a Datavane Account.
5.3 **Non-Solicitation of Datavane Accounts.** During the Term and for a period of twenty-four (24) months following expiration or termination of this Agreement for any reason, Partner shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit, market to, or provide any implementation, migration, managed or consulting services to any Datavane Account in connection with any cloud data warehouse, lakehouse, data platform or analytics platform other than the Datavane Platform.
5.4 **Transition of Accounts.** Upon expiration or termination, Partner shall, at Datavane's request, provide reasonable cooperation in transitioning all Partner Services for Datavane Accounts to Datavane or to a partner designated by Datavane.

## 6. FEES AND REVENUE SHARE

6.1 **Joint Deal Revenue Share.** Joint Deal Revenue shall be shared between the Parties in the proportion of eighty percent (80%) to Datavane and twenty percent (20%) to Partner, irrespective of whether Platform subscription fees or Partner Services fees are the larger component of such Joint Deal.
6.2 **Billing.** Datavane shall invoice Datavane Accounts for all Joint Deal Revenue, unless Datavane agrees in writing that Partner may invoice Partner Services directly, in which case Partner shall remit Datavane's share within thirty (30) days of Partner's invoice date.
6.3 **Payment to Partner.** Datavane shall pay Partner's share of Joint Deal Revenue within sixty (60) days after the end of the calendar quarter in which Datavane receives payment from the relevant Datavane Account.
6.4 **Referral Fee.** For Prospects referred by Partner that purchase a Platform subscription without Partner Services, Datavane shall pay Partner a referral fee of eight percent (8%) of first-year net subscription fees actually received by Datavane.
6.5 **Taxes.** Each Party shall bear its own taxes. Any withholding tax required by law shall be deducted from payments and the paying Party shall provide the relevant withholding certificate.
6.6 **Currency.** All amounts under this Agreement are in United States Dollars.
6.7 **Audit.** Datavane may, upon fifteen (15) days' notice, audit Partner's records relating to Joint Deals and Partner Services.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 **Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years, and thereafter renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless Datavane gives notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term (the "**Term**").
7.2 **Termination for Convenience by Datavane.** Datavane may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon fifteen (15) days' written notice to Partner.
7.3 **No Termination for Convenience by Partner.** Partner acknowledges the investment made by Datavane in enabling Partner and agrees that Partner may not terminate this Agreement for convenience during the initial term.
7.4 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after written notice. Datavane may terminate immediately upon written notice if Partner breaches Section 3.3, 3.4 or 5.
7.5 **Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination: (a) all rights granted to Partner shall terminate; (b) Partner shall cease use of Datavane's Marks and return or destroy Datavane's Confidential Information; (c) no revenue share or referral fee shall be payable in respect of any Joint Deal or referral not fully paid by the relevant Datavane Account before the effective date of termination; and (d) Sections 1, 5.1, 5.3, 5.4, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 13 shall survive.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND MARKS

8.1 Datavane retains all right, title and interest in the Platform, its documentation, the Partner Portal and all related intellectual property.
8.2 Datavane grants Partner a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence during the Term to use Datavane's Marks in the Territory solely to promote the Platform in accordance with Datavane's brand guidelines.
8.3 Partner grants Datavane a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use Partner's name and logo in Datavane's partner directory and marketing materials.
8.4 Partner hereby grants Datavane a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use any feedback, connectors, templates or implementation accelerators that Partner submits to the Datavane Partner Marketplace.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by a Party that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential, including information relating to Datavane Accounts.
9.2 Each Party shall hold the other's Confidential Information in confidence, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers with a need to know who are bound by obligations at least as protective.
9.3 The obligations in this Section 9 do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the recipient, was lawfully known before disclosure, is lawfully received from a third party without restriction, or is independently developed.
9.4 These obligations continue for three (3) years after termination, except for trade secrets and information relating to Datavane Accounts, which shall be protected for so long as they remain confidential.

10.1 Each Party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement.
10.2 Partner warrants that Partner Services shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner by appropriately certified personnel.
10.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, THE PLATFORM, SANDBOX AND ALL DATAVANE MATERIALS ARE PROVIDED "AS IS", AND DATAVANE DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Partner.** Partner shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Datavane, its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers, employees and agents from and against any and all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) the Partner Services; (b) any breach of this Agreement by Partner or its Affiliates; (c) any representation made by Partner regarding the Platform; (d) any claim by a Datavane Account relating to Partner Services or any Joint Deal; or (e) any act or omission of Partner's personnel or subcontractors. Partner's obligations under this Section 11.1 shall not be subject to any limitation or cap.
11.2 **By Datavane.** Datavane shall defend Partner against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by Datavane, infringes a United States patent or copyright, and shall pay damages finally awarded, subject to Section 12.2.
11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defence and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Exclusion.** IN NO EVENT SHALL DATAVANE BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE, BUSINESS OR GOODWILL, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.
12.2 **Cap on Datavane Liability.** DATAVANE'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, INCLUDING UNDER SECTION 11.2, SHALL NOT EXCEED TEN THOUSAND UNITED STATES DOLLARS (USD 10,000).
12.3 **Partner Liability.** NOTHING IN THIS AGREEMENT SHALL LIMIT PARTNER'S LIABILITY UNDER SECTION 5, SECTION 9 OR SECTION 11.1.

## 13. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

13.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods shall not apply.
13.2 **Venue.** Any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the City and County of San Francisco, California, and each Party irrevocably submits to the personal jurisdiction of such courts and waives any objection based on inconvenient forum.
13.3 **Injunctive Relief.** Datavane may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Sections 5, 8 or 9.

## 14. GENERAL

14.1 **Relationship.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise, employment or agency relationship.
14.2 **Assignment.** Partner may not assign this Agreement, by operation of law, change of control or otherwise, without Datavane's prior written consent. Datavane may assign this Agreement without consent.
14.3 **Amendments to Program Terms.** Datavane may update the Global Partner Program Guide, certification requirements and Partner Portal terms from time to time upon notice to Partner, and such updates shall form part of this Agreement.
14.4 **Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered by courier or email to the addresses above: if to Datavane, Attn: Rachel Kim, VP Global Partnerships, with a copy to legal@datavane.com; if to Partner, Attn: Director.
14.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, except for payment obligations.
14.6 **Severability; Waiver.** If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall continue in effect. No waiver is effective unless in writing.
14.7 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with the Global Partner Program Guide, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements relating to its subject matter.
14.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
