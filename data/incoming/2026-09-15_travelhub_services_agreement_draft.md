1.2 **AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation, with its principal office at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, NJ 08540, USA ("**AtliQ**" or "**Developer**").

## 2. RECITALS

A. TravelHub operates a consumer travel planning app and web platform ("**TravelHub App**") that helps travellers in the Middle East plan trips, build itineraries, and book hotels, experiences and transfers with TravelHub's partners.

B. TravelHub wishes to engage AtliQ to design and build certain AI-driven features for the TravelHub App, and AtliQ is willing to do so on the terms set out below.

## 3. DEFINITIONS

3.1 "**Partner Hotels**" means hotels, resorts and serviced apartments that have entered into a commercial or commission agreement with TravelHub for the listing and booking of their inventory through the TravelHub App.
3.2 "**Deliverables**" means the software modules, models, APIs, source code, documentation and other work product described in Clause 4 and Schedule A.
3.3 "**Customer Data**" means all data provided by TravelHub or generated by users of the TravelHub App, including booking history, search behaviour and user profiles.
3.4 "**Acceptance**" means TravelHub's written confirmation that a Deliverable meets the acceptance criteria in Schedule A.
3.5 "**Background Technology**" means software, tools and know-how owned by AtliQ prior to the Effective Date or developed independently of this Agreement.

## 4. SCOPE OF WORK

4.1 AtliQ shall design, develop, test and deploy the following modules into the TravelHub App production environment:

   (a) **Itinerary Personalization Engine** — a recommendation service that generates personalised day-by-day itineraries using user preferences, trip purpose, budget, travel dates, past bookings and in-app behaviour, and re-ranks suggested activities, restaurants and experiences in real time;

   (b) **Partner Hotel Ranking Module** — a ranking and recommendation service that scores, ranks and surfaces Partner Hotels in the United Arab Emirates and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia to TravelHub users at the point of search and itinerary building, taking into account user fit, price competitiveness, partner commission tier, availability and conversion likelihood, and that drives the sort order of hotel results and "recommended stay" placements for booking through the TravelHub App;

   (c) **Partner Performance Dashboard** — a dashboard for TravelHub's partnerships team showing impressions, click-through, bookings and commission by Partner Hotel, city and ranking position; and

   (d) **Model Operations** — deployment pipelines, monitoring and a monthly retraining process for the models in (a) and (b).

4.2 The initial launch markets for the Partner Hotel Ranking Module shall be Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh and Jeddah, with further cities in the UAE and KSA to be added by configuration.
4.3 AtliQ shall provide a technical lead and a team of engineers of appropriate seniority. AtliQ's team may work remotely but shall be available for a daily stand-up during Gulf Standard Time business hours.
4.4 TravelHub shall provide access to its cloud environment, API documentation, historical booking data and a product owner with decision-making authority.
