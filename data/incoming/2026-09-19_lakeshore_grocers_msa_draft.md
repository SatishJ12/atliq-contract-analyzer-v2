# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is made as of ____________, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") between:

**Lakeshore Grocers Inc.**, an Illinois corporation having its principal office at 222 West Wacker Drive, 14th Floor, Chicago, Illinois 60606 ("**Lakeshore**"); and

**AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation having its principal office at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Vendor**").

## 1. BACKGROUND

1.1 Lakeshore operates a chain of 58 neighborhood grocery stores across Illinois, Wisconsin and Indiana, and is a wholly owned subsidiary of Lakeshore Holdings Pte Ltd, a company incorporated in Singapore ("**Parent**").
1.2 Lakeshore wishes to modernize its retail analytics function and has selected Vendor to provide data engineering and business intelligence services under this Agreement and one or more Statements of Work.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a document executed by both parties describing specific Services, Deliverables, milestones and fees, and referencing this Agreement.
2.2 "**Services**" means the professional services performed by Vendor under an SOW.
2.3 "**Deliverables**" means the tangible work product delivered to Lakeshore under an SOW.
2.4 "**Milestone Fee**" means the portion of the Fees allocated to a milestone in an SOW.
2.5 "**Lakeshore Data**" means all data provided by or on behalf of Lakeshore, including point-of-sale, loyalty, inventory and supplier data.
2.6 "**Rate Card**" means the hourly rates set out in Exhibit A.

## 3. SERVICES

3.1 Vendor shall perform the Services described in each SOW. The first SOW ("**SOW-1**") is attached as Exhibit B and covers:
   (a) migration of Lakeshore's legacy on-premises SQL Server data warehouse to a cloud lakehouse;
   (b) build of automated ingestion pipelines for point-of-sale, loyalty, inventory and e-commerce order data from all stores;
   (c) a certified semantic layer for sales, margin, shrink and promotions; and
   (d) Power BI dashboards for store operations, category management and executive leadership.
3.2 Vendor shall staff the Services with personnel of the roles and seniority set out in the applicable SOW. Vendor may use offshore delivery personnel provided that Lakeshore Data containing personal information is accessed only through Lakeshore-approved secure environments.
3.3 Lakeshore shall provide timely access to systems, data and subject-matter experts and shall designate a project sponsor (initially Tom Okafor, Director of Analytics).
3.4 Changes to an SOW shall be made only by a written change order signed by both parties. Additional work under a change order shall be charged at the Rate Card unless otherwise agreed.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Lakeshore shall pay the Fees set out in each SOW. The Fees for SOW-1 total USD 64,000, payable by milestone as set out in Exhibit B.
4.2 Time-and-materials work, change orders and any additional SOWs shall be charged at the rates set out in the Rate Card, which shall remain fixed for the first twenty-four (24) months of this Agreement.
4.3 Vendor shall invoice upon completion of each milestone. Lakeshore shall pay undisputed amounts within thirty (30) days of receipt of invoice.
4.4 Pre-approved travel expenses shall be reimbursed at cost in accordance with Lakeshore's travel policy.
4.5 Fees are exclusive of applicable sales and use taxes, which Lakeshore shall pay except for taxes based on Vendor's net income.

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years, renewing automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives sixty (60) days' notice of non-renewal.
5.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.
5.3 Lakeshore may terminate any SOW for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice, and shall pay Vendor for all Services performed through the effective date of termination.
5.4 Termination of this Agreement shall not affect any SOW then in effect, which shall continue to be governed by this Agreement until completed or terminated.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform its obligations under this Agreement, and not disclose it except to employees, contractors and advisers with a need to know who are bound by similar obligations.
6.2 "Confidential Information" excludes information that is public, previously known, independently developed or rightfully received from a third party.
6.3 These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 Upon payment in full, Lakeshore shall own the Deliverables created specifically for Lakeshore under an SOW.
7.2 Vendor retains all rights in its pre-existing tools, code libraries and methodologies ("**Vendor Tools**"). Vendor grants Lakeshore a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use any Vendor Tools incorporated in the Deliverables for Lakeshore's internal business purposes.
7.3 Lakeshore retains all rights in Lakeshore Data.

## 8. WARRANTIES

8.1 Vendor warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards.
8.2 Vendor warrants that the Deliverables will materially conform to the applicable SOW for ninety (90) days after acceptance and will re-perform non-conforming Services at no charge.
8.3 EXCEPT AS SET OUT ABOVE, VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS.
9.2 EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE UNDER THE APPLICABLE SOW IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM, EXCEPT FOR BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY, INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 Vendor shall defend and indemnify Lakeshore against third-party claims alleging that the Deliverables infringe a U.S. patent, copyright or trademark or misappropriate a trade secret.
10.2 Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage to the extent caused by its negligence or willful misconduct.

## 11. DELAY

11.1 If Vendor fails to complete a milestone by the date specified in the SOW due to causes attributable to Vendor, Lakeshore may assess liquidated damages of one-half percent (0.5%) of the affected Milestone Fee per week of delay, up to a maximum of five percent (5%) of that Milestone Fee.
11.2 Liquidated damages shall not apply to any delay caused by Lakeshore, its third-party vendors or a force majeure event.

## 12. DATA SECURITY

12.1 Vendor shall comply with Lakeshore's Information Security Requirements (Exhibit C) and applicable privacy laws, including the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act.
12.2 Vendor shall notify Lakeshore within seventy-two (72) hours of discovering any unauthorized access to Lakeshore Data.
12.3 Upon completion of the Services, Vendor shall return or securely delete Lakeshore Data.

## 13. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

13.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.
13.2 The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation between senior executives for thirty (30) days.
13.3 Any dispute not so resolved shall be finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre ("**SIAC**") in accordance with the SIAC Arbitration Rules then in force. The seat of arbitration shall be Singapore. The tribunal shall consist of one arbitrator. The language of the arbitration shall be English. This forum is selected to align with the dispute resolution framework applicable across the Parent's group of companies.
13.4 Nothing in this Section prevents either party from seeking interim injunctive relief from a court of competent jurisdiction.

## 14. GENERAL

14.1 **Independent Contractor.** Vendor is an independent contractor and is solely responsible for its personnel.
14.2 **Non-Solicitation.** Neither party shall solicit the other's employees engaged in the Services during the term and for twelve (12) months thereafter.
14.3 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except that Lakeshore may assign to Parent or any Parent affiliate.
14.4 **Insurance.** Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability ($1,000,000) and professional liability ($2,000,000) insurance.
14.5 **Notices.** Notices shall be sent in writing to the addresses above: to Lakeshore, Attn: Tom Okafor, with a copy to Legal Department; to Vendor, Attn: President.
14.6 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, with its Exhibits and SOWs, is the entire agreement between the parties.
14.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed electronically and in counterparts.
