# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**AtliQ Inc — Ref. ATQ-US-NDA-2026-011**

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of ____________, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") between **AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**AtliQ**"), and **Daniel Ortiz**, an individual residing at 4718 Avenue F, Austin, Texas 78751 ("**Mr. Ortiz**"). AtliQ and Mr. Ortiz are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 The Parties wish to exchange information in connection with a proposed and ongoing engagement of Mr. Ortiz as an independent contractor providing data engineering services to AtliQ (the "**Purpose**").

## 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including business plans, client names and project details, pricing, source code, technical designs, and (in the case of Mr. Ortiz) his rates and proprietary tools.

2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was lawfully known to it before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS

3.1 The Receiving Party shall (a) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose; (b) not disclose it to any third party except, in AtliQ's case, to its Affiliates, employees and professional advisers with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement; and (c) protect it with at least reasonable care.

3.2 The Receiving Party shall promptly notify the Disclosing Party of any unauthorized use or disclosure of which it becomes aware.

3.3 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, provided it gives prompt prior notice where lawful and discloses only what is required.

## 4. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

4.1 On the Disclosing Party's request, or at the end of the engagement, the Receiving Party shall return or delete the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information and confirm in writing that it has done so, except for copies required to be kept by law.

## 5. TERM

5.1 This Agreement applies to information disclosed during the two (2) years following the Effective Date. The obligations in Sections 3 and 4 continue for three (3) years after the end of that period.

## 6. NO LICENSE

6.1 Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party, and no license is granted except as expressly stated. Ownership of work product created in the engagement is governed by the Parties' Independent Contractor Agreement.

## 7. REMEDIES

7.1 Each Party may seek injunctive relief for any actual or threatened breach of this Agreement, in addition to any other remedy.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. The Parties submit to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Travis County, Texas.

8.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter, may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties, and may not be assigned by either Party without the other's consent, except by AtliQ to an Affiliate or successor.

8.3 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
