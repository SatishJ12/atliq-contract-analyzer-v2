## RECITALS

A. The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship concerning the development of data analytics and reporting solutions for the Company's lending and wealth businesses (the "**Proposed Transaction**").

B. In connection with the Proposed Transaction, the Parties anticipate a mutual exchange of information, and wish to set out the terms on which such information will be protected.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a Party.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all information, in any form, disclosed or made available by or on behalf of the Company to the Recipient or its Representatives, whether before or after the Effective Date, including customer and portfolio data, credit models, product plans, pricing, technology architecture, business processes, and all analyses, compilations, notes or other documents prepared by the Recipient that contain or reflect such information.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's directors, officers, employees and professional advisers.

1.4 "**Residuals**" means information retained in the unaided memory of individuals who have had access to information exchanged in connection with the Proposed Transaction, including ideas, concepts, know-how and techniques.

1.5 "**Competitor**" means any bank, non-banking financial company, wealth manager, broker or fintech platform operating in India that offers products substantially similar to those of the Company.

## 2. USE AND PROTECTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 The Recipient shall:
   (a) use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating and, if applicable, performing the Proposed Transaction;
   (b) keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential and not disclose it to any person other than its Representatives who need to know it and are bound by obligations at least as protective as this Agreement;
   (c) apply security measures no less stringent than those required under the Company's information-security policy as notified to the Recipient from time to time; and
   (d) be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

2.2 The Recipient shall not, without the Company's prior written consent, disclose to any person the fact that discussions are taking place or any of their terms.

## 3. EXCEPTIONS

3.1 The obligations in Clause 2 shall not apply to information which the Recipient can demonstrate by contemporaneous written evidence:
   (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach by the Recipient or its Representatives;
   (b) was in the lawful possession of the Recipient prior to disclosure, free of any obligation of confidence; or
   (c) is lawfully received by the Recipient from a third party not under any obligation of confidence to the Company.

3.2 If the Recipient is required by law or by any regulator to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall give the Company prior written notice and disclose only such part as it is legally required to disclose.

## 4. RESIDUALS

4.1 Notwithstanding anything in this Agreement, the Company shall be free to use Residuals for any purpose, including the development, marketing and procurement of products and services, provided that this Clause 4.1 does not grant any licence under any patent or copyright.

## 5. RETURN OF INFORMATION

5.1 On the Company's written request, the Recipient shall promptly, and in any event within five (5) business days, return or permanently destroy all Confidential Information and certify such destruction in writing signed by a director.

## 6. REGULATORY MATTERS

6.1 The Recipient acknowledges that the Company is regulated by the Reserve Bank of India and the Securities and Exchange Board of India, and agrees to provide such co-operation and access to records as the Company or its regulators may reasonably require in connection with the Confidential Information.

6.2 The Recipient shall comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in respect of any personal data forming part of the Confidential Information, and shall notify the Company of any personal data breach within six (6) hours.

## 7. DURATION

7.1 This Agreement shall come into force on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated by either Party on thirty (30) days' written notice.

7.2 The obligations of the Recipient under this Agreement shall survive termination and shall continue in perpetuity.

## 8. PROTECTIVE COVENANTS

8.1 In order to protect the Confidential Information, the Recipient shall not, during the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months after its termination, provide services similar to those contemplated by the Proposed Transaction to any Competitor.

8.2 The Recipient shall not, during the term of this Agreement and for a period of twenty-four (24) months after its termination, directly or indirectly solicit, hire or engage any employee or consultant of the Company.

## 9. NO REPRESENTATION; NO OBLIGATION

9.1 The Confidential Information is provided "as is" and the Company makes no warranty as to its accuracy or completeness.

9.2 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to proceed with the Proposed Transaction.

9.3 No licence under any intellectual property right of the Company is granted by this Agreement.

## 10. REMEDIES

10.1 The Recipient acknowledges that damages alone would not be an adequate remedy for breach of this Agreement and that the Company shall be entitled to injunctive relief and specific performance, without proof of actual damage, in addition to any other remedy.

10.2 The Recipient shall indemnify the Company for all losses, costs and expenses (including legal fees) arising from any breach of this Agreement by the Recipient or its Representatives.
