## 1. SERVICES

1.1 The Contractor shall provide the services described in the Engagement Schedule (the "**Services**"). Additional services may be added by a written Engagement Schedule signed by both Parties.
1.2 The Contractor shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards, and shall comply with the Company's reasonable instructions regarding deliverables and timelines.
1.3 The Contractor determines the manner, means and hours of performing the Services, subject to reasonable coordination with the Company's project team.

## 2. TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the term stated in the Engagement Schedule, unless terminated earlier under this Section 2.
2.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for any reason on fourteen (14) days' written notice to the other Party.
2.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within ten (10) days after receiving written notice of it.
2.4 On termination, the Company shall pay the Contractor for all Services performed through the effective date of termination, and the Contractor shall deliver to the Company all work product completed or in progress.

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Company shall pay the Contractor at the hourly rate stated in the Engagement Schedule.
3.2 The Contractor shall submit a monthly invoice with a summary of hours worked. The Company shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt, by ACH transfer in U.S. Dollars.
3.3 If the Company disputes any portion of an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Contractor within ten (10) days of receipt and pay the undisputed portion on time.
3.4 Pre-approved, reasonable out-of-pocket expenses shall be reimbursed at cost against receipts.

## 4. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

4.1 The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner or agent of the Company. The Contractor is not eligible for any Company employee benefits.
4.2 The Contractor is solely responsible for all taxes on amounts paid under this Agreement. The Contractor shall provide a completed IRS Form W-9, and the Company shall issue IRS Form 1099-NEC as required.
4.3 The Contractor may provide services to other clients during the term, provided that doing so does not interfere with the Services or breach Section 7.

## 5. EQUIPMENT AND TOOLS

5.1 The Contractor shall furnish, at the Contractor's own expense, the computer equipment, software, internet connectivity and other tools the Contractor considers necessary to perform the Services, and shall be responsible for maintaining them.
5.2 The Company shall provide the Contractor with user accounts and access credentials to the Company's project repositories and communication tools as needed for the Services. The Contractor shall not share those credentials with any other person.

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 All work product, code, scripts, data pipelines, documentation and other materials created by the Contractor in performing the Services (the "**Work Product**") are works made for hire for the Company to the extent permitted by law. To the extent any Work Product is not a work made for hire, the Contractor hereby assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in it, including all intellectual property rights, effective upon payment for the Services to which it relates.
6.2 The Contractor retains ownership of general skills, know-how and tools developed before the Effective Date and not specific to the Company or its clients ("**Contractor Tools**"). If any Contractor Tools are incorporated into Work Product, the Contractor grants the Company and its clients a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive, transferable license to use them as part of the Work Product.
6.3 The Contractor shall execute any documents reasonably required to perfect the Company's rights under this Section 6, at the Company's expense.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential the non-public business, technical and financial information of the other Party, and of the Company's clients, disclosed to it in connection with this Agreement, and shall use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.
7.2 The obligations in Section 7.1 do not apply to information that is publicly available, already known to the receiving Party, lawfully received from a third party, or independently developed.
7.3 The Parties have entered into, or will enter into, a Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement, which shall also apply. On termination, the Contractor shall return or delete Company and client information in the Contractor's possession.
7.4 This Section 7 survives termination for three (3) years.

## 8. NON-SOLICITATION

8.1 During the term and for six (6) months after it, neither Party shall knowingly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom it worked in connection with the Services. General advertisements not targeted at such persons are not a breach. Nothing in this Agreement restricts the Contractor from working for any client or competitor of the Company after the term.

## 9. WARRANTIES AND LIABILITY

9.1 The Contractor warrants that the Work Product will be original to the Contractor (except for Contractor Tools and open-source components disclosed to the Company) and will not knowingly infringe any third party's intellectual property rights.
9.2 Each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable to the Contractor under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, except for breach of Section 7 or a Party's fraud or willful misconduct.
9.3 Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, incidental or consequential damages.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Governing Law; Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Travis County, Texas.
10.2 **Assignment.** The Contractor may not assign or subcontract this Agreement without the Company's prior written consent. The Company may assign this Agreement to an Affiliate or successor on written notice.
10.3 **Notices.** Notices shall be given in writing by email with confirmation of receipt, or by courier, to the addresses above (for the Company, Attn: President).
10.4 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, including the Engagement Schedule, is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties.
10.5 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
