## RECITALS

WHEREAS, AtliQ provides data engineering, analytics and AI/ML services to its clients, and requires certain data preparation and annotation services in connection with a client project;

WHEREAS, AtliQ desires to engage Vendor to provide such services, and Vendor desires to provide such services, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**AtliQ Materials**" means all data, datasets, information, systems access, documents and other materials furnished by or on behalf of AtliQ or the End Client.
1.2 "**Deliverables**" means all labelled datasets, annotations, label files, quality reports, documentation and other materials prepared or delivered by or on behalf of Vendor under this Agreement, in whole or in part.
1.3 "**Delivery Date**" means each date for delivery of a Deliverable set forth in the Engagement Schedule.
1.4 "**End Client**" means the client of AtliQ identified in the Engagement Schedule for whose project the Services are performed.
1.5 "**Fees**" means the amounts payable to Vendor as set forth in the Engagement Schedule.
1.6 "**Losses**" means any and all losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, claims, actions, judgments, settlements, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs or expenses of whatever kind, including reasonable legal fees.
1.7 "**Services**" means the services described in Article 2 and in the Engagement Schedule.
1.8 "**Total Fees**" means the aggregate Fees payable under this Agreement as stated in the Engagement Schedule.
1.9 "**Vendor Personnel**" means all partners, employees, agents and contractors of Vendor who perform any portion of the Services.

## ARTICLE 2 — SERVICES

2.1 **Scope.** Vendor shall perform the Services described in the Engagement Schedule.
2.2 **Standards of Performance.** Vendor shall perform the Services in a diligent, professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the highest standards of the industry, and in compliance with AtliQ's annotation guidelines and quality requirements made available to Vendor, as updated from time to time.
2.3 **Vendor Personnel.** Vendor shall assign qualified Vendor Personnel. AtliQ may, in its sole discretion, request removal of any Vendor Personnel, and Vendor shall promptly replace such individual at no additional cost to AtliQ.
2.4 **Subcontracting.** Vendor shall not subcontract any portion of the Services without AtliQ's prior written consent. Vendor shall remain fully responsible for any subcontractor's performance.
2.5 **Change Orders.** No change in the scope of Services shall be binding unless set forth in a written change order executed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

## ARTICLE 3 — ACCEPTANCE

3.1 AtliQ shall have fifteen (15) days following delivery of each Deliverable to test and evaluate such Deliverable against the acceptance criteria in the Engagement Schedule, including where AtliQ so elects, review by the End Client.
3.2 If AtliQ determines that a Deliverable does not conform, Vendor shall correct the non-conformity at no cost within five (5) business days. AtliQ may repeat the acceptance process until the Deliverable conforms, or may, at its option, reject the Deliverable and receive a refund of all Fees paid in respect thereof.
3.3 Acceptance shall not be deemed to occur by use, by delivery to the End Client, by payment, or by the passage of time.

## ARTICLE 4 — FEES, INVOICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** AtliQ shall pay Vendor the Fees set forth in the Engagement Schedule. Fees are firm and fixed and shall not be increased during the term of this Agreement.
4.2 **Invoices.** Vendor shall invoice AtliQ monthly in arrears for Deliverables accepted in the preceding month. Each invoice shall be a valid tax invoice under the CGST Act, 2017, state Vendor's GSTIN and PAN, and reference the Agreement No.
4.3 **Payment Terms.** AtliQ's obligation to pay any invoice is conditional upon AtliQ's receipt of payment from the End Client for the corresponding services. AtliQ shall pay each properly submitted, undisputed invoice within fifteen (15) days after AtliQ receives the corresponding payment from the End Client, and in any event not later than one hundred and twenty (120) days after AtliQ's receipt of the invoice.
4.4 **Disputed Amounts.** AtliQ may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith. Vendor shall continue performing the Services during the pendency of any dispute.
4.5 **Set-off.** AtliQ may set off against any amount owed to Vendor any amount owed by Vendor to AtliQ under this Agreement, including liquidated damages.
4.6 **Taxes.** GST shall be payable in addition to the Fees against a valid tax invoice. AtliQ shall deduct tax at source as required under the Income-tax Act, 1961 and furnish the applicable TDS certificate.

## ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 **Term.** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until completion of the Services, unless earlier terminated as provided herein.
5.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days after written notice thereof.
5.3 **Termination for Convenience.** AtliQ may terminate this Agreement, in whole or in part, for any reason or no reason, upon seven (7) days' written notice to Vendor. Upon such termination, AtliQ's sole obligation shall be to pay Fees for Deliverables accepted by AtliQ prior to the effective date of termination; AtliQ shall have no obligation to pay for work in progress, unaccepted Deliverables, demobilization costs or anticipated profits.
5.4 **Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination, Vendor shall immediately deliver to AtliQ all Deliverables, whether complete or incomplete, and return or delete all AtliQ Materials.
5.5 **Survival.** Articles 6, 7, 9, 10, 11 and 12 shall survive expiration or termination.

6.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of AtliQ or the End Client, including AtliQ Materials, business plans, technical information and pricing.
6.2 Vendor shall (a) hold Confidential Information in strict confidence, (b) use it only to perform the Services, and (c) disclose it only to those Vendor Personnel with a need to know who are bound by written obligations at least as protective.

## ENGAGEMENT SCHEDULE

**End Client:** Rheinwerk Analytics GmbH, Munich, Germany (AtliQ project: Rheinwerk Insight data pipeline modernisation, Contract No. RWA-2026-014).

**Services:** Cleansing, labelling and annotation of machine-sensor time-series data and maintenance records exported from the End Client's Rheinwerk Insight platform, to populate the cleansed and curated data layers and data quality framework of the End Client project (Milestone M3), including training and validation datasets for the End Client's predictive-maintenance models. Source data comprises vibration, temperature and pressure sensor streams from End Client customer plants, together with maintenance work orders and technician logs (including technician names, shift identifiers and free-text notes). Vendor Personnel shall access data via AtliQ-provisioned cloud workspaces and Vendor's own labelling platform.
