## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 The Parties intend to discuss a possible engagement for AtliQ to build a customer analytics and demand-forecasting solution for LoopMart's stores and online storefront (the "**Purpose**"). Each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other for the Purpose.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by a Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential, including sales and customer data, supplier terms, pricing, product roadmaps, solution designs, source code and methodologies.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS

3.1 The Receiving Party shall:
   (a) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
   (b) disclose it only to its employees, directors, professional advisers and subcontractors who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement;
   (c) protect it with at least reasonable care and no less care than it applies to its own confidential information; and
   (d) promptly notify the Disclosing Party of any unauthorised use or disclosure of which it becomes aware.

3.2 Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by persons to whom it discloses Confidential Information.

## 4. EXCLUSIONS

4.1 Clause 3 does not apply to information that:
   (a) is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement;
   (b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure;
   (c) is lawfully obtained from a third party without restriction; or
   (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

4.2 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or a competent authority, after giving the Disclosing Party prompt notice where legally permitted, and disclosing only what is required.

## 5. PERSONAL DATA

5.1 Where Confidential Information includes personal data, the Receiving Party shall process it only for the Purpose and in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## 6. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 Within fifteen (15) days of a written request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, except for copies held in automatic back-ups or required to be retained by law, which remain confidential.

## 7. TERM

7.1 This Agreement remains in force for two (2) years from the Effective Date. Either Party may terminate it earlier on thirty (30) days' written notice.

7.2 Each Party's obligations under Clauses 3 to 6 continue for three (3) years after expiry or termination of this Agreement.

## 8. OWNERSHIP AND NO OBLIGATION

8.1 Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence is granted except the limited right to use it for the Purpose.

8.2 Neither Party is obliged to enter into any further agreement. Each Party remains free to work with third parties, provided it does not use or disclose the other Party's Confidential Information.

## 9. REMEDIES

9.1 Each Party acknowledges that damages may not be an adequate remedy for breach, and the Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies available at law.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties.

10.2 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent.

10.3 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

10.4 This Agreement may be executed electronically and in counterparts.
