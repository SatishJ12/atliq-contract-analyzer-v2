## 2. BACKGROUND

A. BlueOrchid owns, operates and manages a portfolio of twelve (12) business and leisure hotels in India and the Middle East under the "BlueOrchid" and "Orchid Residences" brands.

B. BlueOrchid receives a large volume of guest reviews and feedback across online travel agencies, review websites, social media and its own post-stay surveys, which are currently reviewed manually by each property.

C. BlueOrchid wishes to run a time-bound pilot of an AI-based guest review analytics solution across its entire portfolio, and AtliQ has agreed to provide the same on the terms of this Agreement.

## 3. DEFINITIONS

3.1 "**Pilot**" means the guest review analytics pilot described in Clause 4 and Annexure A.
3.2 "**Properties**" means the twelve (12) hotels listed in Annexure B.
3.3 "**Deliverables**" means the solution, dashboards, models, reports and documentation delivered by AtliQ under this Agreement.
3.4 "**Milestone Fee**" means the fee attributable to a Milestone as set out in Annexure C.
3.5 "**Client Data**" means all review data, survey responses, booking references and other data made available by BlueOrchid, including any personal data contained therein.
3.6 "**Pre-existing IP**" means any software, tools, libraries, frameworks, methodologies and know-how owned or developed by a Party prior to or independently of this Agreement.

## 4. SCOPE OF SERVICES

4.1 AtliQ shall deliver a guest review analytics solution for all twelve (12) Properties listed in Annexure B, comprising:
   (a) automated ingestion of guest reviews from up to six (6) online sources and BlueOrchid's post-stay survey tool;
   (b) multilingual sentiment and aspect analysis (English, Hindi, Arabic) covering room, cleanliness, F&B, service, location and value;
   (c) property-level and portfolio-level dashboards in Power BI for General Managers, the Guest Experience team and senior management;
   (d) a weekly automated "Voice of Guest" summary for each Property; and
   (e) an alerting mechanism flagging recurring negative themes for the relevant Property's General Manager.
4.2 The Pilot shall run for a period of sixteen (16) weeks from the Effective Date, comprising a twelve (12) week build and rollout phase and a four (4) week evaluation phase.
4.3 BlueOrchid shall nominate a single point of contact (initially Ms. Farah Sayed, Director – Guest Experience) and shall provide timely access to data sources, systems and personnel reasonably required by AtliQ.
4.4 Any change in scope shall be documented through a written change request agreed by both Parties.
