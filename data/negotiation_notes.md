# Past negotiations — what we asked for, what we gave (reconstruction)

*(Compiled by Jay on 26–27 Sep 2026 after the contract-queue meeting, by searching Karandeep's, Dhaval's and Bhavin's inboxes for old redline threads. Not complete — Jay stopped when the Harrington draft needed attention. Quotes are copied from the emails. Nobody has reviewed this list.)*

---

**Crestline Retail (US) — MSA, Jul 2024 — AtliQ Inc**
- They added §6.4 (pricing): "Supplier represents and warrants that the rates, fees and other commercial terms offered to Crestline … are and shall remain no less favorable than those offered by Supplier or its Affiliates to any other customer for substantially similar services…"
- Dhaval asked K. K: "We never discount, so it's harmless — fine."
- Accepted as drafted. Rate card: Sr Data Engineer $85/hr, Data Engineer $62/hr, BI Developer $58/hr. Signed 1 Aug 2024.

**PayTrack (India) — MSA, Jan 2025**
- Their indemnity: we indemnify for everything "including those arising from the negligence of PayTrack".
- K: "Can't accept 10.1 as written — we can't insure against PayTrack's own negligence." Proposed mutual indemnity (each side covers its own breach/negligence). Liability cap stays 1x fees.
- They accepted mutual. Signed 22 Jan 2025.

**Rohan Iyer (freelancer, Power BI) — contractor agreement, Apr 2025**
- Freelancer template. Rohan asked to keep IP in "the Work Product" and give us a licence ("my visuals library is the only thing I really own as a freelancer").
- Dhaval said fine; K replied "ok" (from phone).
- Signed 28 Apr 2025. He worked on the Acme dashboards.

**Acme Retail (India) — MSA, Mar 2025**
- Their delay clause: LD 1% of Contract Value per week, no cap.
- K countered: 0.5% of the affected milestone fee per week of delay attributable to us, max 5% of that milestone fee, no LD for delays caused by client. "Open-ended isn't something we can sign."
- Accepted. Signed 10 Mar 2025. Indian law, Mumbai arbitration.

**Al Noor Hospitality (Dubai) — MSA, Mar 2025 — Pvt Ltd**
- Non-compete: they asked worldwide, 3 years. K: "Worldwide for three years would stop us working in hospitality altogether." Landed on GCC, term + 18 months.
- LD: they asked 1% of total fees per week capped at 10% of total fees; wouldn't go below 10% cap. We got the weekly 1% calculated on the milestone instead of total fees.
- Bhavin: "I say take it. $185k, 14 properties, first Gulf logo. And on §12 — we'll never do another hotel in the Gulf anyway. I approve the 10% as a one-off."
- K: "Approving 10% of total fees as a strategic exception for Al Noor only — not our new standard."
- DIFC law / DIFC courts — accepted ("group standard for all technology vendors").
- Signed 15 Mar 2025.

**Northwind Logistics (UK) — MSA + SOW-1, Jun 2025 — Pvt Ltd**
- Their paper. LD 10% of Contract Value if go-live missed → K countered 0.5%/week of milestone, cap 5% of milestone, client delays excluded. Agreed.
- Asked for Indian law / Mumbai arbitration. Tom: board policy, English law non-negotiable. K: "English law / LCIA — not worth losing the deal over. Rest looks ok, I skimmed the IP and boilerplate."
- SOW-1 uses PipeKit. Signed 20 Jun 2025.

**CloudSpan (Singapore) — partnership agreement, Aug 2025 — Pvt Ltd**
- Their exclusivity: we (and Affiliates) can't partner with any "Competing Platform" in India, the GCC or Europe.
- K: "We can't do Europe — Europe is where we're growing." Asked for mutual 90-day termination for convenience.
- Agreed: India + GCC only, mutual 90 days. 12% referral fee, co-marketing, 4 certified engineers (Pranav confirmed). Singapore law / SIAC ok.
- Signed 1 Sep 2025.

**CareBridge Clinics (US healthcare) — NDA + MSA + BAA, Aug–Oct 2025 — AtliQ Inc**
- BAA sent to Calloway Stern (Diane Foster) for review — $3,800.
- K asked Pranav to set up an intercompany BAA (AtliQ Inc ↔ Pvt Ltd) so Pune can work on de-identified data. Diane: could be per engagement or a master. K: "Per engagement, keep it simple."
- NDA signed 11 Aug 2025; MSA and BAAs signed 6 Oct 2025. Only de-identified data offshore.

**PixelCraft Studio (design agency, 6 people) — our vendor agreement, Oct 2025**
- Ishaan pushed back on three clauses in our template: 1% per day delay penalty, 60-day payment, unlimited vendor liability.
- K: "He's right. They're six people. I don't want our paper to be the thing I hate receiving." Deleted the penalty, 30-day payment, liability capped at fees for both sides.
- Signed 20 Oct 2025. (Changes made in PixelCraft's copy only — not sure the template file was ever updated.)

**Brightwater Insurance (US) — Services Agreement, Nov 2025 — AtliQ Inc**
